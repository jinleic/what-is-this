## 4. AI-assisted and computational announcements (2025–2026)

- **AlphaEvolve Ramsey paper — arXiv:2603.09172**, "Reinforced Generation of Combinatorial
  Structures: Ramsey Numbers", Ansh Nagda, Prabhakar Raghavan, Abhradeep Thakurta
  (v1 2026-03-10 ... v5 2026-04-21). URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09172
  Abstract (verbatim): "We present improved lower bounds for nine classical Ramsey numbers:
  R(3,13) is increased from 60 to 61, R(3,18) from 99 to 100, R(4,13) from 138 to 139, R(4,14)
  from 147 to 148, R(4,15) from 158 to 159, R(4,16) from 170 to 174, R(4,18) from 205 to 209,
  R(4,19) from 213 to 219, and R(4,20) from 234 to 237. These results were achieved using
  AlphaEvolve, an LLM-based code mutation agent. Beyond these new results, we successfully
  recovered lower bounds for all Ramsey numbers known to be exact, and matched the best known

  elsewhere; the R(5,5) >= 43 lower bound stands.

## 5. Conjecture R(5,5) = 43 and the 656 Ramsey(5,5,42) graphs

- DS1 rev 18 (April 2026) still presents the conjecture unchanged (quote in Section 1 above):
  656 critical graphs on 42 vertices, conjectured complete [MR5, 1997].
- **Brendan McKay's Ramsey graph data page** (fetched 2026-08-13):
  https://users.cecs.anu.edu.au/~bdm/data/ramsey.html
  Verbatim: "Geoffrey Exoo found several Ramsey(5,5,42)-graphs in 1989. McKay and Radziszowski
  expanded this to 656 graphs and conjectured that there are none larger. However, there could
  be more with 42 vertices and even some with 43-47 vertices. r55_42some.g6 contains 328 of
  these graphs; the other 328 are their complements."
  Note: the page still says "43-47 vertices", i.e. it has not even been updated for the <= 46
  bound (which would cap candidates at 45 vertices) — strong evidence no new (5,5,42) graphs or
  larger candidates have been posted there.
- Wikipedia (Ramsey's theorem, fetched 2026-08-13) [REPORTED — secondary source]:
  "The exact value of R(5,5) is unknown, although it is known to lie between 43 (Geoffrey Exoo
  (1989)) and 46 (Angeltveit and McKay (2024)), inclusive." And: "In 1997, McKay, Radziszowski
  and Exoo employed computer-assisted graph generation methods to conjecture that R(5,5) = 43.
  They were able to construct exactly 656 (5,5,42) graphs... None of the 656 graphs can be
  extended to a (5,5,43) graph." URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramsey%27s_theorem
- **No new 42-vertex Ramsey(5,5) graphs and no 43-vertex candidates found in any 2025–2026
  source.** The 2022 study of Exoo's construction (arXiv:2212.12630, "Study of Exoo's Lower
  Bound for Ramsey number R(5,5)") predates the window and did not raise the bound.
  URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.12630
