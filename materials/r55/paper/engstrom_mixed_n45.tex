\title{A machine-certified exhaustion of the first exact identity basis for
hypothetical Ramsey$(5,5,45)$ graphs, and the measured obstruction that closes it}

\author{J.\ Chen\thanks{Computational campaign; all artifacts, sources and
verification transcripts accompany this note.}}

\date{21 August 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We report the terminal outcome of an exact, fully machine-verified linear
programming campaign over the finite state relaxation of a hypothetical
Ramsey$(5,5,45)$ graph. The relaxation carries one interval row per
subgraph-counting identity: the McKay--Radziszowski (MR97) rows at $m=2,3,4$ and
Engstr\"om's non-separable degree-4 neighbourhood/anti-neighbourhood identity,
the only identity of the campaign's recorded first exact basis that had never
been used inside any computational certificate. Adding the Engstr\"om row
produces \emph{no} accepted structural consequence: all three instantiable
routes are rejected by exact rational primal witnesses, so the recorded basis is
exhausted at $n=45$. We claim nothing about $\Rams(5,5)$.

The contribution is not the negative disposition but its mechanism, which we
measure exactly. First, the new row is genuinely new geometrically: the rank of
the state-endpoint matrix rises from $6$ to $8$ when the Engstr\"om endpoints are
adjoined, refuting the natural hypothesis that projection onto the $n=45$ domain
collapses its coordinates. Second, it nonetheless has \emph{zero} cut strength:
the exact dual and primal optima of all three routes are bit-identical with and
without it ($360$, $14310/349$, $45$), and every optimal certificate carries
$\eta=\theta=0$. Third, we localise the loss: it is the per-coordinate interval
abstraction, not the identity or the projection. The median per-state Engstr\"om
window is wider than the median magnitude of the row itself (ratio $1.207$),
its aggregate constraints are strictly slack at every optimal witness, and on a
complete catalog class the discarded joint structure is a
$\rho=0.9464$-correlated point cloud replaced by its bounding box.

Fourth, we show that fixing any of this would not help. Although the envelope
certification of the $77$ catalog-missing stratum classes is $111\times$ looser
than catalog truth and carries $98.6\%$ of all window width, collapsing every
such window to a \emph{point} still leaves the three routes at $360$, $39.25$,
$38.36$ against acceptance edges $315$, $1$, $1$: a factor-$\approx39$ gap
survives perfect data. The obstruction is not window quality but the absence of
vertex-to-vertex coupling.

Fifth, we therefore build the level-2 pair-coupled lift and close it too. With
adjacent and non-adjacent degree-pair variables, the per-degree-class
neighbour-degree identity and three exact codegree bounds, the optima move to
$360$, $41$, $45$: two routes unchanged, one improved by $1/349$. The
explanation is exact --- the campaign's $m=2$ row \emph{is} the degree-squared
identity $\sum_v\deg(v)^2=45e+\sum_v e(G[N(v)])-\sum_v e(G[D(v)])$ in disguise,
so every handshake-style repair was already present. Levels $1$ and $2$ of the
first exact basis at $n=45$ are thus both closed. We also record, with its
regression test, a near-miss row that is independent of the basis and appears to
cut hard but is unsound: exact certificates prove optimality for the programme
written, not soundness of the programme.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}

Write $\Rams(5,5)\in\{43,\dots,46\}$ (Exoo; Angeltveit--McKay
\cite{angeltveit2024}). At $n=45$ the only published structural restriction on a
hypothetical Ramsey$(5,5,45)$ graph is the degree window
$d_v\in\{20,\dots,24\}$. Our campaign works inside a finite relaxation of that
setting, built from complete published $\Rams(4,5)$ censuses.

\begin{definition}[State relaxation]\label{def:cone}
For a hypothetical Ramsey$(5,5,45)$ graph $G$ and a vertex $v$ put
$d=d_v$, $m=44-d$, let $X=G_v^{+}$ be the neighbourhood, $Y=G_v^{-}$ the
anti-neighbourhood, and $Z=\overline{Y}$. Both $X$ and $Z$ are
$\Rams(4,5)$ graphs, so $e(X)=E_{45}(d)-a$ and $e(Z)=E_{45}(m)-b$ for
edge deficiencies $a,b\ge0$, where $E_{45}$ is the published maximum edge count.
The \emph{state} of $v$ is $(d,a,b)$; there are exactly $3{,}215$ feasible
states. Each state carries exact integer intervals for four rows: the $m=2$
excess balance (a single integer), the $m=3$ row $g$, the $m=4$ row $h$, and the
Engstr\"om mixed row $F$ of Section~\ref{sec:row}.
\end{definition}

Because every vertex occupies exactly one state, $G$ induces nonnegative
rational weights $w_s$ with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:relax}
\sum_s w_s = 45,\qquad
\sum_s w_s\,\mathrm{bal}(s)=0,\qquad
\sum_s w_s r_{lo}(s)\ \le\ 0\ \le\ \sum_s w_s r_{hi}(s)
\quad (r\in\{g,h,F\}),
\end{equation}
since each identity sums to zero over the $45$ vertices while each vertex row
lies in its own state window. All statements below concern this relaxation only.

\section{The Engstr\"om row}\label{sec:row}

Engstr\"om \cite{engstrom2010} proved the MR97 subgraph-counting conjecture,
yielding for every graph $G$ on $n$ vertices the universal identity
$\sum_v\big[p_1(G_v^{+})+p_2(G_v^{-})+p_3(G_v^{+},G_v^{-})\big]=0$ with
\begin{align*}
p_1(X)&=n(n-3)s(K_1,X)-(n^2+2n-6)s(K_1,X)^2+3n\,s(K_1,X)^3-2s(K_1,X)^4\\
&\quad+2(n^2+n-8)s(K_2,X)-12s(K_2,X)^2-12(n-1)s(K_1,X)s(K_2,X)+12s(K_1,X)^2s(K_2,X)\\
&\quad+72s(C_4,X)+12(n-2)s(K_3,X)+24s(K_{1,3},X)+24s(P_4,X)+24s(T_{3,1},X)\\
&\quad+12(n+2)s(P_3,X)-24s(K_1,X)s(P_3,X)+32s(T_{3,2},X),\\
p_2(Y)&=4s(K_2,Y)^2-12s(K_{1,3},Y)-8s(C_4,Y)-8s(T_{3,1},Y)-24s(T_{3,2},Y)+2(n-8)s(P_3,Y),\\
p_3(X,Y)&=4s(K_1,X)s(P_3,Y)-2(n-2)s(K_1,X)s(K_2,Y)+4s(K_1,X)^2s(K_2,Y),
\end{align*}
where $s(J,H)$ counts induced copies, $T_{3,1}$ is the paw and $T_{3,2}$ the
diamond. Two features matter here. It is \emph{non-separable} (the $p_3$ cross
terms), hence outside the MR97 Lemma 2.3 classification of separable identities
of degree $\le6$; and its coordinate support
($C_4,K_{1,3},P_3,P_4$ on both sides, plus three mixed products) is disjoint
from the frozen basis support $\{d,e,t,\mathrm{paw},\mathrm{diamond},i_4\}$.
It requires no $K_4$-free hypothesis. To our knowledge it had never been used
inside a computational certificate for any Ramsey claim.

Specialising to $n=45$ and eliminating the $Y$-side by the complement map
$s(P_3,\overline Z)=\mathrm{pc}_3(Z)$, $s(C_4,\overline Z)=s(2K_2,Z)$,
$s(K_{1,3},\overline Z)=s(K_3{+}K_1,Z)$,
$s(T_{3,1},\overline Z)=s(P_3{+}K_1,Z)$,
$s(T_{3,2},\overline Z)=s(K_2{+}2K_1,Z)$, $s(P_4,\overline Z)=s(P_4,Z)$ gives the
per-vertex row used throughout, with $e_y=\binom{44-d}{2}-e_z$:
\begin{align}\label{eq:F}
F_v &= 1890d-2109d^2+135d^3-2d^4+4124e_x-12e_x^2-528\,d\,e_x+12d^2e_x
      +4e_y^2-86\,d\,e_y+4d^2e_y\notag\\
&\quad +516\,t(x)+72\,c_4(x)+24\,\mathrm{claw}(x)+24\,p_4(x)+24\,\mathrm{paw}(x)
      +(564-24d)\,p_3(x)+32\,\mathrm{diamond}(x)\notag\\
&\quad +(74+4d)\,\mathrm{pc}_3(z)-12\,k_3k_1(z)-8\,(2K_2)(z)-8\,(P_3{+}K_1)(z)
      -24\,(K_2{+}2K_1)(z).
\end{align}

\paragraph{Kernel validation.} The eleven raw induced $4$-vertex counters, the
complement map and \eqref{eq:F} are verified by exhaustive comparison against
naive $4$-subset enumeration on all $33{,}867$ labelled graphs with $n\le6$,
spot checks at $n\in\{7,8\}$, residual zero on all $328$ published
Ramsey$(5,5,42)$ representatives \emph{and} their complements ($656$ checks),
and replay of the $n=49$ type-combination values
$\{0,144,288,432\}$ with a unique zero corner on the frozen order-$24$ census.
Six plausible closed forms are provably wrong and are pinned as detected
mutations with named witnesses (e.g.\ $\mathrm{claw}=\sum_v\binom{d_v}{3}-\mathrm{paw}$
fails on the $4$-vertex diamond; $s(C_4,\overline Z)=s(C_4,Z)$ fails on $2K_2$;
dropping $+c_{uv}$ from $\mathrm{pc}_3$ fails on $K_3$).

\paragraph{Interval endpoints.} Per-class exact motif windows come from a single
streaming pass over $8{,}500{,}211$ graphs (the $36$ validated edge-extremal
$\Rams(4,5)$ catalogs at orders $17$--$23$ plus the complete order-$24$ census),
recording exact histograms of fifteen aggregates and asserting $K_4$-freeness on
every graph. Classes absent from the catalogs receive sound outer windows from a
certified envelope LP over the R1--R5 relation system (raw $4$-motif
coordinates) or from degree-histogram enumeration (the $3$-motif-derived
aggregates). Every observed catalog value is verified to lie inside its outer
window. Since \eqref{eq:F} splits additively over the two strata, interval
endpoints are obtained by sign-aware affine images of the per-coordinate windows.

\section{Certificates, witnesses, and the terminal result}

\begin{definition}[Eight-coefficient certificate]
A tuple $(\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta,\varepsilon,\zeta,\eta,\theta)$ with
$\gamma,\delta,\varepsilon,\zeta,\eta,\theta\ge0$ such that for every state $s$
\[
\mathrm{obj}(s)\ \le\ \alpha+\beta\,\mathrm{bal}(s)
+\gamma g_{lo}(s)-\delta g_{hi}(s)
+\varepsilon h_{lo}(s)-\zeta h_{hi}(s)
+\eta f_{lo}(s)-\theta f_{hi}(s).
\]
Weighting by any $w$ admissible for \eqref{eq:relax} gives
$\sum_v \mathrm{obj}\le 45\alpha$; we additionally require $\alpha$ to be the
least constant its own coefficients admit, so certificates are canonical.
\end{definition}

Three routes are frozen in advance, with acceptance edges fixed before any run:
\texttt{total\_deficiency} (accepted at $45\alpha\le315$), \texttt{degree20\_count}
and \texttt{deficiency\_ge8\_count} (accepted at $45\alpha<1$, which forces a
nonnegative integer count to vanish). A fourth route,
\texttt{required\_local\_family}, is not instantiable in this repository (no
hash-pinned complete gluing-cover manifest exists) and is recorded with no
evidence at all. A route is refuted when an exactly rational admissible $w$
attains a value beyond the same edge.

\begin{theorem}[Terminal result]\label{thm:main}
Over the relaxation of Definition~\ref{def:cone} with all four rows, every
instantiable route is refuted by an exact rational primal witness:
\[
\texttt{total\_deficiency}: 360,\qquad
\texttt{degree20\_count}: \tfrac{14310}{349},\qquad
\texttt{deficiency\_ge8\_count}: 45,
\]
each equal to the exactly verified least upper bound $45\alpha$ of the same
route. Consequently no certificate over
$\{\mathrm{bal},g,h,F\}$ can accept any frozen route, and the campaign's
recorded first exact basis (MR97 $m=2,3,4$ plus the Engstr\"om identity) is
exhausted at $n=45$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Verification]
Coefficients are proposed by floating-point LP, then discarded: each is
rationalised on a frozen denominator ladder, $\alpha$ is re-derived exactly as
$\max_s(\cdot)$, and the affine inequality is re-checked at all $3{,}215$
states in exact rational arithmetic. Primal supports are proposed numerically,
then re-solved exactly by \texttt{Fraction} Gaussian elimination over every
active/inactive choice of the six interval inequalities; each witness is
re-verified against \eqref{eq:relax} including the two new aggregate conditions.
Route statuses and the terminal disposition are re-derived from verified
evidence, never read from storage. An independent verifier that imports none of
the producers re-hashes all $64$ recorded inputs, re-derives the
$8{,}500{,}211$ catalog counts across $53$ classes, recomputes all $3{,}215$
$F$ intervals from the artifact's own windows using its own affine arithmetic,
and re-derives every bound, witness value, status and the disposition. A
twelve-mutation corruption battery (endpoints, padded and lowered $\alpha$,
witness weights, trust roots, histogram counts, replay fields, route status,
deleted state, schema version, swapped window pair, input digest) is rejected
item-by-item while the untouched artifact passes.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
Theorem~\ref{thm:main} is a statement about a relaxation, not about
$\Rams(5,5)$. It refutes routes; it proves no bound. In particular nothing here
supports or contradicts $\Rams(5,5)\le45$.
\end{remark}

\section{The measured obstruction}

The negative outcome had two candidate explanations: the new row might be
redundant after projection (``coordinate collapse''), or it might be
informative but wasted by the relaxation. We separate them exactly.

\begin{proposition}[The row is geometrically new]\label{prop:rank}
Over the $3{,}215$ states,
\[
\rank\big[\mathbf 1,\mathrm{bal},g_{lo},g_{hi},h_{lo},h_{hi}\big]=6,\qquad
\rank\big[\cdots,f_{lo}\big]=7,\qquad
\rank\big[\cdots,f_{lo},f_{hi}\big]=8 .
\]
Hence neither Engstr\"om endpoint is an affine combination of the frozen
endpoints: the collapse hypothesis is false.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Yet it has zero cut strength]\label{prop:strength}
For each frozen route the exact optimum of the dual programme and of the primal
programme is unchanged when the two $F$ columns, respectively the two $F$
inequalities, are deleted:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lcccc}
\toprule
route & dual (m4 basis) & dual (mixed) & primal (m4) & primal (mixed)\\
\midrule
\texttt{total\_deficiency} & $360$ & $360$ & $360$ & $360$\\
\texttt{degree20\_count} & $14310/349$ & $14310/349$ & $14310/349$ & $14310/349$\\
\texttt{deficiency\_ge8\_count} & $45$ & $45$ & $45$ & $45$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Every optimal certificate carries $\eta=\theta=0$; e.g.\ the
\texttt{degree20\_count} optimum is $(\alpha,\beta,\delta)=(318/349,6/349,1/1047)$
with all other coefficients zero.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Mechanism: the interval abstraction]\label{prop:mech}
The two new aggregate constraints are strictly slack at every optimal witness,
with
\[
\textstyle\sum_s w_sf_{lo}(s)=-\tfrac{18522745}{2},\quad
-\tfrac{2245549320}{349},\quad -11622240
\]
for the three routes respectively (and correspondingly large positive
$\sum_s w_sf_{hi}$). The slack is structural: per-state window widths are
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrrrr}
\toprule
row & min & median & mean & max\\
\midrule
$g$ & $0$ & $930$ & $986$ & $1{,}458$\\
$h$ & $0$ & $41{,}128$ & $41{,}808$ & $72{,}078$\\
$F$ & $864$ & $802{,}396$ & $764{,}953$ & $1{,}318{,}306$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
so the median $F$ window ($802{,}396$) exceeds the median $|F|$ magnitude
($664{,}870$). The row's information is destroyed by per-coordinate
intervalisation, not by the identity and not by the projection.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[The discarded structure is large and measurable]\label{prop:corr}
On the complete catalog class $(22,114)$ ($133$ graphs), the $x$-side
contributions to the $h$ and $F$ rows satisfy
$h_x\in[1686,2056]$, $F_x\in[234048,239156]$ and
\[
\rho(h_x,F_x)=0.9464 .
\]
The box relaxation replaces this near-degenerate cloud by its
$370\times5108$ bounding box.
\end{proposition}

Propositions~\ref{prop:rank}--\ref{prop:corr} together give a sharper negative
than a bare disposition: the frozen basis is exhausted \emph{as parameterised by
independent per-row intervals}, and the exhaustion is caused by a quantified
loss of joint information rather than by exhaustion of the identities.

\section{Window quality is not the bottleneck}\label{sec:notwindows}

Propositions~\ref{prop:mech}--\ref{prop:corr} suggest tightening the relaxation:
each row splits additively over the two strata,
$r=r_{\text{exact}}(d,a,b)+r_x(x)+r_z(z)$ for $r\in\{g,h,F\}$, so the attainable
set of the \emph{triple} $(g,h,F)$ is a Minkowski sum of two finite point
clouds and the present relaxation replaces it by a bounding box. Three such
repairs suggest themselves: convex hulls over the catalog clouds; structural
extremal caps on the catalog-missing classes (e.g.\ Zykov's theorem gives
$i_4(z)\le900$ at order $22$ against the envelope's $1285$); and exact joint
projection of the envelope polytope. We measured all three, and all three are
dead ends.

\begin{proposition}[Where the width lives]\label{prop:where}
Summed over all $3{,}215$ states, the $h$ and $F$ window width contributed by
catalog-certified stratum classes is $35{,}649{,}683$ ($1.4\%$); by the $77$
envelope-LP classes, $2{,}558{,}092{,}854$ ($98.6\%$). Hence the catalog-side
correlation of Proposition~\ref{prop:corr} governs only $1.4\%$ of the width,
and hull-tightening over catalog clouds cannot matter. Separately, exact joint
projection of the envelope polytope onto the $(h_z,F_z)$ plane for the class
$(22,107)$ tightens the area only by a factor $1.5$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[The envelope is $111\times$ loose, and it still does not
matter]\label{prop:shrink}
On the five catalog-present extremal classes the envelope LP is $111\times$
looser than the truth in aggregate width ($92{,}888$ against $836$; e.g.\
$i_4$ at $(22,114)$ is truly $[67,99]$ against the LP's $[0,1285]$).
Nevertheless, recomputing every $h$ and $F$ endpoint from the artifact's own
window table while shrinking all envelope-class windows toward their midpoints
by a factor $k$ (holding $g$ at its frozen values; at $k=1$ the recomputation
reproduces all $3{,}215$ recorded intervals exactly) gives
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lccc}
\toprule
$k$ & \texttt{total\_deficiency} & \texttt{degree20\_count} & \texttt{deficiency\_ge8\_count}\\
 & (edge $\le315$) & (edge $<1$) & (edge $<1$)\\
\midrule
$1$ & $360$ & $41.003$ & $45.000$\\
$0.1$ & $360$ & $39.352$ & $38.632$\\
$0.01$ & $360$ & $39.260$ & $38.387$\\
$0$ & $360$ & $39.251$ & $38.363$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
with primal $=$ dual throughout; at $k=0$ the primal still places $39.251$ of
the $45$ vertex-weight on degree-$20$ states.
\end{proposition}

Even a \emph{perfect} motif certification --- every catalog-missing class pinned
to a point --- therefore leaves a factor-$\approx39$ gap on the count routes. No
data-quality campaign can close it. (The rows $k<1$ are a sensitivity analysis,
not a theorem: the shrunken windows are centred at current midpoints and are not
sound certifications. The rigorous row is $k=1$; the robustness is nonetheless
decisive, three orders of magnitude of width reduction buying $1.75$ units on a
gap of $38$.)

\paragraph{What the obstruction is.} The relaxation imposes exactly five
aggregate conditions on $3{,}215$ nonnegative weights, so an optimal vertex has
support at most five, and five linear conditions cannot separate the degree-$20$
states: the programme is free to place $\approx39$--$41$ of the $45$ weight on
them. The parameterisation is not at fault; the \emph{absence of vertex-to-vertex
coupling} is. The relaxation never records that neighbourhoods of adjacent
vertices intersect. A cheap candidate repair also fails: combining
$\sum_s w_sd_s=2e$ with the averaged per-vertex inequality
$e\ge d_v+e(N_v)+e(D_v)$ is slack by $\approx200$ at every state, since
$e\approx495$ while the right-hand side is $\approx250$.

\paragraph{More constraints, not better data.} What is missing is vertex-to-vertex
coupling: a level-2 Sherali--Adams lift carrying pair variables with marginal
consistency to the present $w_s$. We built it, and it is closed.

\section{The level-2 pair lift is closed}\label{sec:lift}

The lift adjoins, for each integer edge count $e$, ordered degree-pair variables
$M[d,d']$ (adjacent) and $N[d,d']$ (non-adjacent), symmetric and nonnegative,
with marginals $\sum_{d'}M[d,d']=d\,n_d$ and $\sum_{d'}N[d,d']=(44-d)n_d$ where
$n_d=\sum_{d_s=d}w_s$; the per-degree-class neighbour-degree identity
\eqref{eq:nbr} below; the per-state window $20d_s\le e+\varphi(s)\le24d_s$; and
three codegree facts, writing $\lambda(u,v)=|N(u)\cap N(v)|$. For $u\sim v$,
$N(u)\cap N(v)$ is $K_3$-free with independence number $\le4$, so
$\lambda\le R(3,5)-1=13$. For $u\not\sim v$, $D(u)\cap D(v)$ has independence
number $\le2$ and is $K_5$-free, so
$|D(u)\cap D(v)|=43-d_u-d_v+\lambda\le R(5,3)-1=13$, giving the \emph{upper}
bound $\lambda\le d_u+d_v-30$; and $\lambda\ge\max(0,d_u+d_v-43)$ since
$N(u)\cup N(v)$ omits $u$ and $v$. These tie to the vertex layer through
$\sum_v e(G[N(v)])=3T$ and
$\sum_{u\not\sim v}\lambda=\sum_v\binom{d_v}{2}-3T$.

\begin{proposition}[The pair lift does not open the routes]\label{prop:lift}
Maximised over every integer $e$ for which the lifted programme is feasible,
which is exactly $e\in[454,536]$, the exact optima are $360$, $41$ and $45$
against frozen $360$, $14310/349$ and $45$. Two routes are unchanged; the third
improves by $1/349$. No route reaches its acceptance edge.
\end{proposition}

The reason is a collapse we can state exactly. For any finite simple graph, pure
double counting gives $\sum_{u\in N(v)}\deg(u)=d_v+2e(G[N(v)])+e_{xz}(v)$ and
$e=d_v+e(G[N(v)])+e(G[D(v)])+e_{xz}(v)$, hence for every vertex
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nbr}
\sum_{u\in N(v)}\deg(u)=e+e(G[N(v)])-e(G[D(v)]),
\end{equation}
and, summing and using $\sum_v\sum_{u\in N(v)}\deg(u)=\sum_u\deg(u)^2$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:deg2}
\sum_v\deg(v)^2=45e+\sum_v e(G[N(v)])-\sum_v e(G[D(v)]).
\end{equation}
We verified \eqref{eq:nbr} and \eqref{eq:deg2} on all $2{,}131{,}018$ labelled
graphs up to $n=7$ and on random graphs at $n=12,20,45$.

\begin{proposition}[The $m=2$ row \emph{is} the degree-squared identity]\label{prop:collapse}
Adjoining \eqref{eq:deg2} to \eqref{eq:relax} gains nothing, because it is
already present. In this repository the $z$ stratum is the complement of the
anti-neighbourhood, so $e_z=E_{45}[m]-b$ equals $e(\bar G[D(v)])$ and the
interior count is $e_y=\binom{m}{2}-e_z$; with that substitution
$2d^2-45d-2e_x+2e_y=\mathrm{bal}(s)$ identically on all $3{,}215$ states, and
the exact rank of $\{1,\mathrm{bal},g_{\mathrm{lo}},g_{\mathrm{hi}},
h_{\mathrm{lo}},h_{\mathrm{hi}},f_{\mathrm{lo}},f_{\mathrm{hi}}\}$ is $8$ both
before and after adjunction.
\end{proposition}

Proposition~\ref{prop:collapse} retires the whole family of handshake-,
assortativity- and degree-correlation repairs at the aggregate level, including
the one suggested by \S\ref{sec:notwindows}. Only the per-degree-class form of
\eqref{eq:nbr} is strictly finer than its aggregate, and
Proposition~\ref{prop:lift} prices that refinement at $1/349$.

\paragraph{A recorded trap.} Substituting the recorded $e_z$ directly for
$e(G[D(v)])$ in \eqref{eq:deg2} yields
$r(s)=2d^2-45d-2(E_{45}[d]-E_{45}[m])+2a-2b$, which \emph{is} independent of the
frozen basis (rank $8\to9$) and appears to cut every route hard --- to
$4905/14$, $225/11$, $4905/112$, and under integrality of the degree
distribution to $349$, $20$, $43$ --- with matching exact dual certificates and
exact primal witnesses throughout. All of it is invalid: $r$ misreads the
stratum convention. We record it because exact certificates and matching
witnesses establish optimality \emph{for the programme written down}; they carry
no information about whether that programme is sound. Reading the non-adjacent
bound $\lambda\le d+d'-30$ as a lower bound is a second such trap, and makes
every $e$ infeasible, i.e.\ manufactures a refutation of $R(5,5)=45$. Both are
pinned by regression tests.

\paragraph{Consequence.} Levels $1$ and $2$ of this basis are now both closed,
and the factor-$\approx39$ margin of Proposition~\ref{prop:shrink} shows data
quality cannot substitute for a non-local argument. The recorded residual tiers
---VeriPB-certified gluing search, then $\mathrm{srg}(45,22,10,11)$
complementary SAT---are the only remaining routes.

\section{Reproducibility}

All numbers above are produced by the accompanying sources and artifact.
The canonical artifact \texttt{engstrom\_identity.json} (schema 3) records the
provenance of every input by SHA-256, the fifteen per-class exact histograms,
the per-class catalog and outer windows with their derivation method, all
$3{,}215$ states with interval and motif provenance, the four search records
with exact certificates and witnesses, and the disposition. Commands:

\begin{verbatim}
python -m unittest discover -s r55/tests -p 'test_*mixed*.py' -v
python r55/src/mixed_deficiency_cone.py --output r55/data/engstrom_identity.json
python r55/src/search_mixed_cuts.py     --analysis r55/data/engstrom_identity.json
python r55/src/verify_mixed_independent.py       r55/data/engstrom_identity.json
\end{verbatim}

\paragraph{Trust boundary and remaining gap.} Catalog completeness is assumed
from the published censuses and is named, not re-proved. Floating-point LP output
is used only to propose; every accepted number is exact. The disposition is
producer-verified, independently re-verified at the search and $F$-interval
layer, and cross-anchored to the frozen $m=4$ artifact. Not yet redone
independently: a second motif kernel re-sweeping the $8.5$M graphs, and
recomputation of the $g,h$ endpoints from R1--R5 rather than by equality against
the frozen $m=4$ projection.
