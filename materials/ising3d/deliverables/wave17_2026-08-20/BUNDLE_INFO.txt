ISING3D EXACT-RESEARCH WAVE-17 PAPER AND ARTIFACT BUNDLE
========================================================

Purpose
-------
This directory consolidates three paper drafts and the exact repository artifacts behind the current most important results. The 3D Ising model remains unsolved. Every paper separates THEOREM, LEMMA, COMPUTATION, EXTERNAL, and UNRESOLVED scope.

Papers
------
papers/paperA_no_go_mechanisms.tex / .pdf
  Exact no-go certificates for Dolan--Grady, tridiagonal, claw/Majorana,
  Pauli-string flux, spectral Gaussianity, all-size anisotropic Gaussian,
  tetrahedron/RLLL, and positive-real Kac--Ward mechanisms. The full complex
  scalar Kac--Ward family is explicitly left unresolved.

papers/paperB_series_bounds.tex / .pdf
  Exact high-temperature series through v^28, low-temperature series through
  x^56, finite structure frontiers, and the current rigorous K_c interval.

papers/paperC_layer_DLA.tex / .pdf
  Exact characteristic-zero local-term DLA classifications for 2xL, L=2..5,
  plus the all-L invariant-form containment and dimension upper bound. All-L
  saturation/type equality beyond L=5 is explicitly open.

Provenance and limitations
--------------------------
- K_c benchmark 0.221654626 is comparison-only; it was not used to select or fit results.
- Modular ranks/gcd degrees are used only in their proven one-sided directions.
- Finite series do not prove a closed form, D-finiteness, or a thermodynamic-limit solution.
- The staged docs are a snapshot; executable reproduction should use the repository root.
- MANIFEST.json records byte sizes and SHA-256 hashes for every staged file except MANIFEST.json itself (self-referential hashing is excluded).
