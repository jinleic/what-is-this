\begin{abstract}
We determine the characteristic-zero dynamical Lie algebras generated by the local Pauli terms of open $2\times L$ Ising layers at $L=2,3,4,5$, and prove a uniform all-$L$ containment theorem.  The local-term algebra is distinct from the smaller two-generator algebra generated only by $A=\sum X_i$ and $B=\sum ZZ$.  Exact Pauli closure, parity splitting, invariant signed checkerboard forms, and rational rank certificates give
\[
\g_{2\times2}=\so_8(\mathbb Q)\oplus\so_8(\mathbb Q),\quad
\g_{2\times3}=\sp_{32}(\mathbb Q)\oplus\sp_{32}(\mathbb Q),
\]
\[
\g_{2\times4}=\so_{128}(\mathbb Q)\oplus\so_{128}(\mathbb Q),\quad
\g_{2\times5}=\sp_{512}(\mathbb Q)\oplus\sp_{512}(\mathbb Q).
\]
For every $L\ge2$, the generators lie in the corresponding orthogonal or symplectic product and satisfy an explicit dimension upper bound.  Equality, saturation, maximality, and type classification are asserted only at $L\le5$; the all-$L$ reverse-inclusion problem remains open.
\end{abstract}

\section{The object and a necessary distinction}
Let $n=2L$ and let $\g_L$ be the dynamical Lie algebra over $\Q$ generated by all local terms of the open ladder:
\[
 \{X_i:1\le i\le n\}\ \cup\ \{Z_uZ_v:(uv)\text{ is an open ladder bond}\}.
\]
There are $n$ single-site terms and $3L-2$ bonds.  The algebra acts on $V=\Q^{2^n}$.

This paper studies the local-term algebra.  It must not be confused with the two-generator algebra $\langle A,B\rangle_{\rm Lie}$, where $A=\sum_iX_i$ and $B=\sum_{(uv)}Z_uZ_v$.  For the open $2\times3$ layer the former has dimension $1056$ and type $\sp_{32}\oplus\sp_{32}$, whereas the latter has dimension $263$ and a different semisimple quotient.  Modular $\sp_{14}$, $\sp_6$, and $\gl_6$ witnesses belong to that 263-dimensional two-generator algebra and are not quotients of the 1056-dimensional local-term algebra.

All Pauli brackets are exact.  In the binary encoding $(x\mid z)\in\F_2^{2n}$, a nonzero bracket is the XOR product, up to a nonzero scalar.  Therefore exact BFS closure by Pauli strings gives a rational basis and an exact dimension, with no floating-point rank inference.

\section{Exact characteristic-zero classifications}
\subsection{$L=2$: orthogonal base case}
The open $2\times2$ layer has $n=4$, two product-$X$ parity halves of dimension $8$, and
\[
 \dim\g_2=56=2\dim\so_8.
\]
The exact invariant form on each half is symmetric; the local-term algebra is
\[
 \g_2\cong\so_8(\Q)\oplus\so_8(\Q).
\]
This is the finite control for the alternation below.

\subsection{$L=3$: first symplectic point}
\begin{theorem}[Exact $2\times3$ local-term algebra]
For the six single-site terms and seven open bonds of the $2\times3$ layer,
\[
 \dim_{\Q}\g_3=1056,
 \qquad \g_3\cong\sp_{32}(\Q)\oplus\sp_{32}(\Q).
\]
The algebra is perfect, its diagonal Killing form is nondegenerate with exact signature $(544,512)$, and it splits into two 528-dimensional ideals selected by the two product-$X$ parity representations. Each ideal acts faithfully on one 32-dimensional parity half-module, which carries a nondegenerate alternating rational form.
\end{theorem}

The exact Pauli closure reaches 1056 strings. The two product-$X$ sectors of the 64-dimensional Hilbert space each have dimension 32; the two corresponding ideals each have dimension 528. Exact nonzero minors at two primes certify the two simple ideals. The chain and $2\times2$ controls recover $\mathfrak{sl}_2\oplus\mathfrak{sl}_2$, $\mathfrak{sl}_4$, and $\so_8\oplus\so_8$ in the expected small cases.

\subsection{$L=4$: orthogonal alternation}
\begin{theorem}[Exact $2\times4$ local-term algebra]
For the eight single-site terms and ten open bonds of the $2\times4$ layer,
\[
 \dim_{\Q}\g_4=16256,
 \qquad \g_4\cong\so_{128}(\Q)\oplus\so_{128}(\Q).
\]
The algebra is perfect, has nondegenerate diagonal Killing signature $(8192,8064)$, and splits on the two product-$X$ parity halves.  Each half preserves a nondegenerate symmetric rational form of determinant one, and its image is the full corresponding orthogonal algebra.
\end{theorem}

The tempting $\sp_{128}\oplus\sp_{128}$ analogy is false: the invariant form is symmetric at even $L$.  The two-generator 2x4 algebra is a separate finite object of dimension 2952 and is not the algebra classified here.

\subsection{$L=5$: exact characteristic-zero symplectic point}
\begin{theorem}[Exact $2\times5$ local-term algebra]
For the 23 local terms of the open $2\times5$ layer,
\[
 \g_5\cong\sp_{512}(\Q)\oplus\sp_{512}(\Q),
 \qquad \dim\g_5=262656.
\]
The two ideals act on the product-$X$ parity halves of dimension 512.  Each half carries a nondegenerate alternating signed-checkerboard invariant form, unique up to scalar.
\end{theorem}

The proof reaches exactly 262656 Pauli strings by integer BFS.  The set-law count is
\[
 (2^9-1)2^9+2^{10}=262656,
\]
and every reached string satisfies the law.  The full 134,479,872-equation sign system has zero contradictions and one connected component, giving existence and uniqueness of the alternating form up to scalar.  This is an exact characteristic-zero theorem, not merely the earlier modular probe.

\section{Uniform all-$L$ containment theorem}
Let $J=(1,\ldots,1)\in\F_2^n$ and let $d$ be one checkerboard class of the ladder.  Define
\[
 S_L=\{(x\mid z): z\text{ has even weight},\ z\neq J,\ (J-z)\cdot x=1+z\cdot d\}
\]
with, when $L$ is odd, the additional full fibre $\{(x\mid J):x\in\F_2^n\}$.  On each product-$X$ parity half, pair the class labels by the signed checkerboard matching $s\leftrightarrow s+d$.

\begin{lemma}[Uniform forward closure]
For every $L\ge2$, the set $S_L$ is closed under every nonzero adjoint action of an $X_i$ or a ladder bond $Z_uZ_v$.  Hence
\[
 \g_L\subseteq\operatorname{span}_{\Q}(S_L).
\]
\end{lemma}

The proof has three cases.  An $X_i$ action preserves the affine fibre; a bond action preserves the law because each ladder bond crosses the checkerboard; and the exceptional $z+z_g=J$ case either has zero commutator for even $L$ or lands in the full $z=J$ fibre for odd $L$.

\begin{theorem}[All-$L$ invariant-form containment]
For every $L\ge2$, $n=2L$,
\[
 \g_L\subseteq
 \begin{cases}
 \so_{2^{n-1}}(\Q)\oplus\so_{2^{n-1}}(\Q),&L\text{ even},\\
 \sp_{2^{n-1}}(\Q)\oplus\sp_{2^{n-1}}(\Q),&L\text{ odd},
 \end{cases}
\]
with respect to the signed checkerboard form on the two product-$X$ halves. Consequently,
\[
 \dim\g_L\le 2^{n-1}\bigl(2^{n-1}-(-1)^L\bigr).
\]
At $L=2,3,4,5$ equality holds, and the exact classifications in Section 2 follow.  Saturation/equality for all $L>5$ is not proved.
\end{theorem}

The form-membership condition is the same parity equation as the set law:
\[
 |x|=1+z\cdot x+z\cdot d\pmod2.
\]
The signed matching obeys $\Omega_L^T=(-1)^L\Omega_L$, so it is symmetric for even $L$ and alternating for odd $L$. At the four certified points, dimension equality forces the full classical algebra on each half and the sign-system calculation gives uniqueness of the form.

\section{What remains open}
The all-$L$ reverse inclusion $\operatorname{span}(S_L)\subseteq\g_L$ is the central missing step.  The reviewed path-telescoping construction generates only a four-parameter family per pair, not arbitrary fibre patterns.  Thus $L=6$ and beyond are predictions, not classifications: for example, $L=6$ would predict $\so_{2048}\oplus\so_{2048}$ of dimension $4192256$, but no exact-Q saturation theorem is claimed here.

Other unresolved issues include a uniform proof of form uniqueness, rational isotropy questions outside the explicit split forms, and the relation of this local-term family to the distinct two-generator transfer algebra.  The $3\times3$ layer is outside the $2\times L$ alternation family and enters a different regime.

These finite Lie-algebra classifications do not diagonalize the 3D Ising transfer matrix or solve its thermodynamic limit.  They are structural data and exact constraints on any proposed integrable mechanism.
