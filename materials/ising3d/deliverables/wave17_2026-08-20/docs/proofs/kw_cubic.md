## 2. The dense multiplier-degree-three block

\[

`68,796` columns.  Its 21 basis polynomials have `1,001` sparse terms in

integer support enumeration gives `1,612,152` distinct monomial rows.
These are sizes of the exact sparse block; no dense rational rank equality is
claimed.

Consequently one pivot column per relation may be eliminated without changing

## 4. A nonsingular exact \(\mathbb F_5\) section

Order the remaining 13 coordinates as

```text
u_py_px, u_py_pz, u_py_mz, u_my_px, u_my_mx, u_pz_px, u_pz_mx,
u_pz_py, u_pz_my, u_mz_px, u_mz_mx, u_mz_py, u_mz_my.
```

Set the last seven to `(1,1,1,1,1,4,4)` and take the following complete
point modulo five:

\[
 p=(1,2,3,3,1,1,1,1,1,1,1,4,4)\in(\mathbb F_5^\times)^{13}.
\]

The first six entries are the variables of the six primitive numerator
equations.  Their exact Jacobian modulo five is

\[
J(p)=
\begin{pmatrix}
4&0&0&0&1&3\\
3&0&2&3&4&0\\
2&2&0&4&1&4\\
2&4&1&1&0&0\\
1&1&4&0&4&4\\
4&0&2&0&4&1
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad \det J(p)=2\pmod 5.
\]

**[THEOREM]** The selected six primitive equations have a unique Hensel lift
in this residue class in \(\mathbb Z_5^6\).  Since the primary equations
vanish identically on the thin torus and the ten nonzero three-dimensional
numerators have the stated exact `Q` span, this lift is a
\(\mathbb Q_5\)-point of all 42 construction equations.

**Exact certificate.** Three multivariate Hensel digits give the first six
coordinates modulo \(5^4=625\) as

```text
(226, 197, 98, 63, 161, 216) mod 625.
```

Direct integer modular substitution into all 42 original branch equations
returns 42 zero residues modulo 625.  The nonsingular-Jacobian hypothesis,
not numerical continuation, is what establishes the infinite five-adic
solution.

**[THEOREM]** The 42-equation ideal is proper over `Q`.  Equivalently, there
is no rational Nullstellensatz identity

\[
1=\sum_i g_iF_i
\]

at any multiplier degree for this catalogue.  Otherwise evaluating the
rational identity at the \(\mathbb Q_5\) Hensel point would give \(1=0\).
This theorem is about the displayed finite ideal only; it does not give a
rational weight vector and does not prove a scalar Kac--Ward identity.

## 5. Exact independent holdout falsification

Use the free `3x3x3` box at orders `4,6,8` as a holdout.  At the finite Hensel
lift above, direct exact modular evaluation gives

```text
(k=4, k=6, k=8) = (0, 0, 250) mod 625.
```

Because

\[
250 = 2\cdot 5^3 \not\equiv 0\pmod{5^4},
\]

the order-eight holdout value at the unique local Hensel lift is nonzero in
\(\mathbb Q_5\).  All Laurent denominators are five-adic units in this
residue class, so the modular conclusion applies to the original trace
equations.

**[FALSIFIED]** This particular nonsingular five-adic thin-torus construction
section cannot satisfy the `3x3x3`, order-eight identity.  The result is an
exact local counterexample to treating the 42 construction equations as
sufficient for the holdout; it is not a proof that every branch-`11111`
construction point fails the holdout.

## 6. Elimination and quartic-extension status

**[COMPUTATION]** An exact modular `SymPy` grevlex Gröbner computation over
`F_5` was attempted on a separate six-variable nonsingular section with

```text

```

and solve variables
`u_py_px,u_py_mz,u_my_px,u_my_mx,u_pz_mx,u_mz_mx`.  It was stopped after

for either emptiness or existence.

**[UNRESOLVED]** No explicit point over a quartic extension
\(\mathbb Q(\alpha)\), and therefore no exact algebraic-coordinate holdout
test of such a point, was obtained.  The five-adic certificate does establish
finite-catalogue nonemptiness and a holdout-failing local specialization, but
it is not presented as a quartic parameterization.

## 7. Conclusion

**[THEOREM]** The finite 42-constraint branch-`11111` ideal has no rational
Nullstellensatz certificate at any degree, because it has the exact
five-adic Hensel point above.

**[FALSIFIED]** One explicit nonsingular five-adic section of that construction
variety fails the independent `3x3x3` order-eight condition with certified

**[UNRESOLVED]** The full generic branch, whether there is another point
passing every free-box identity, and the translation-invariant scalar
Kac--Ward family remain undecided.  In particular, no claim is made about an
explicit \(\mathbb Q\)- or quartic-extension solution, all box sizes, or all
orders.
