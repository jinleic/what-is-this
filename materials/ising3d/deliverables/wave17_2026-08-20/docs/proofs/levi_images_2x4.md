# Exact characteristic-zero quotient images of the open 2x4 layer

This note uses **[THEOREM]**, **[LEMMA]**, **[COMPUTATION]**, **[EXTERNAL]**, and
**[UNRESOLVED]** in the repository sense.  Every new assertion here concerns the
single finite open `2x4` layer.  It is neither an all-size theorem nor an exact
solution of the three-dimensional Ising model.

## 1. Results

Let \(\mathfrak g_{\mathbb Q}\) be the rational Lie algebra generated by the two
open-`2x4` layer matrices \(A,B\).  The three commuting layer symmetries split
the configuration module into the eight character sectors labelled `000`
through `111`.

**[THEOREM — exact orthogonal quotient images.]** The `000` sector has a
two-dimensional common zero kernel.  On the exact 42-dimensional rational
quotient, the image of \(\mathfrak g_{\mathbb Q}\) is

\[
 \mathfrak{so}(F_{000},\mathbb Q)
 =\{X:X^T F_{000}+F_{000}X=0\},
 \qquad \dim=861,
\]

where \(F_{000}\) is the displayed nonsingular rational symmetric form in
`results/algebra_structure/levi_images_2x4.json`.  Its absolute Dynkin type is
\(D_{21}\), and its complexification is \(\mathfrak{so}(42,\mathbb C)\).

Each of `010`, `100`, and `110` has a one-dimensional common zero kernel.  On
each exact 27-dimensional rational quotient, the image is the full
\(\mathfrak{so}(F_s,\mathbb Q)\), of dimension 351 and absolute type \(B_{13}\).
After complexification each is \(\mathfrak{so}(27,\mathbb C)\).

Here \(\mathfrak{so}(F_s,\mathbb Q)\) means the full orthogonal algebra of the
specific certified rational form.  We assert its absolute type, not that this
rational form is split.  This distinction has no effect on the complex
Onsager-algebra corollary below.

**[THEOREM — the remaining four sector images.]** Exact rational constituent
bases give

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
 s&\text{constituent dimensions}&\dim\operatorname{im}_s&
          \operatorname{im}_s\\ \hline
001,111&4+4+24&606&\mathbb Qz\oplus\mathfrak{sl}_4\oplus
 \mathfrak{sl}_4\oplus\mathfrak{sl}_{24}\\
011,101&4+28&799&\mathbb Qz\oplus\mathfrak{sl}_4\oplus
 \mathfrak{sl}_{28}.
\end{array}
\]

The line \(\mathbb Qz\) is central in the displayed sector image.  These are
individual quotient images.  They overlap as quotients of
\(\mathfrak g_{\mathbb Q}\) and must not be added to infer the full Levi
algebra.

**[THEOREM — open-2x4 Onsager-quotient no-go.]** The complexified open-`2x4`
layer algebra is not isomorphic to any finite-dimensional quotient of the
Onsager algebra.  No pair of elements which generates the whole layer algebra
can satisfy the normalized Dolan--Grady presentation.  This is an obstruction
to the abstract finite layer Lie algebra, not just to the displayed pair
\((A,B)\).

## 2. Exact rational quotient audit

The starting sector matrices and rational common-kernel construction are those
of `experiments/e49_char0_levi_2x4.py`, but e59 audits every identity used in the
promotion.

For a sector module \(V\), e59 solves

\[
 K=\ker A\cap\ker B=\ker\begin{pmatrix}A\\B\end{pmatrix}
\]

exactly over \(\mathbb Q\).  It verifies \(AK=BK=0\), so in particular \(K\)
is invariant under both generators.  It extends the computed kernel basis by
standard coordinate vectors to an exact rational basis matrix
\(C=[K\;Q]\), computes \(C^{-1}\) over \(\mathbb Q\), and checks

\[
 C(C^{-1}GC)=GC,
 \qquad (C^{-1}GC)_{*,K}=0
 \quad(G=A,B).
\]

The lower-right blocks are therefore the exact induced actions on \(V/K\), not
matrices reconstructed from a finite field.  They agree entry-for-entry with
e49's quotient matrices.
For all four induced pairs, e59 also recomputes
\(\ker\bar A\cap\ker\bar B=0\), so no common zero submodule remains in the
quoted 42- or 27-dimensional action.


For `000`, `010`, `100`, and `110`, the symmetric-form equations

\[
 G^T F+FG=0\qquad(G=\bar A,\bar B)
\]

are solved over \(\mathbb Q\).  In every case their solution space is
one-dimensional, and the stored integer generator has full rank (42 or 27).
Both displayed containment residuals are recomputed as exact zero matrices.
The exact basis matrices, their inverses, quotient generators, and forms have
canonical row-major rational SHA-256 records.  At the good prime used below,
e59 separately checks that the reductions of \(C\) and \(C^{-1}\) remain
mutual inverses and that the form remains nonsingular.  Thus the modular lower
calculation uses the same quotient basis as the rational upper calculation.

## 3. Why the modular minor is a rational lower bound

**[LEMMA — same-basis Lie-word minor.]** Let \(G_0,G_1\) be rational matrices
whose entry denominators are prime to a prime \(p\).  Suppose literal
right-normed words

\[
 w_1,\ldots,w_r\in\{G_0,G_1,[G_i,w_j]\}
\]

have a nonzero \(r\)-row minor after entrywise reduction modulo \(p\).  Then the
same \(r\) rational word matrices are linearly independent over \(\mathbb Q\).

*Proof.* All entries of every word lie in the localization
\(\mathbb Z_{(p)}\), because addition, multiplication, and brackets introduce
no denominator divisible by \(p\).  If the rational minor vanished, its image
under reduction \(\mathbb Z_{(p)}\to\mathbb F_p\) would vanish.  The displayed
nonzero residue is a contradiction. \(\square\)

**[COMPUTATION — explicit selected minors.]** E59 uses \(p=65521\).  Its rows
are not modularly synthesized basis vectors: every accepted raw row retains a
recipe `seed A`, `seed B`, `[A,parent]`, or `[B,parent]`.  Echelon reduction only
selects rows.  If \(c_i\) is the newly selected coordinate and \(d_i\ne0\) is
the unnormalised insertion pivot, the raw-word matrix restricted to the
columns \((c_1,\ldots,c_r)\) is changed by determinant-one earlier-row
operations to an upper triangular matrix with diagonal \((d_1,\ldots,d_r)\).
Consequently its determinant is \(\prod_i d_i\), exactly the nonzero residue
stored in the artifact.  The artifact stores every word recipe, every selected
coordinate, every insertion pivot, and SHA-256 digests of both the recipe list
and all raw modular rows.

For all eight image calculations the two reduced generator denominator LCMs
are `[1,1]`.  The decisive summaries are:

| sector(s) | rational upper bound | modular minor size | determinant mod 65521 | maximum word depth |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `000` | 861 | 861 | 6248 | 17 |
| `010` | 351 | 351 | 1304 | 15 |
| `100` | 351 | 351 | 47530 | 15 |
| `110` | 351 | 351 | 7018 | 15 |
| `001` | 606 | 606 | 52245 | 16 |
| `111` | 606 | 606 | 13276 | 16 |
| `011` | 799 | 799 | 12576 | 21 |
| `101` | 799 | 799 | 12576 | 21 |

A single good-prime nonzero minor is used only for the lower bound.  Equality
always comes from an independent exact characteristic-zero upper bound for the
same representation and basis.

## 4. Dimension squeeze for the orthogonal images

**[LEMMA — orthogonal ambient dimension.]** If \(F\) is a nonsingular symmetric
\(n\)-by-\(n\) matrix over a field of characteristic zero, then

\[
 \dim\{X:X^TF+FX=0\}=\frac{n(n-1)}2.
\]

*Proof.* Multiplication by \(F\) maps this space bijectively to the
skew-symmetric matrices: \(X\mapsto FX\). \(\square\)

The exact form identities therefore bound the four rational image dimensions
by

\[
 \binom{42}{2}=861,
 \qquad
 \binom{27}{2}=351.
\]

The same-basis minors give the reverse inequalities.  Hence each inclusion in
its orthogonal ambient algebra is equality.  Over \(\mathbb C\), the
42-dimensional case is the simple algebra of type \(D_{21}\), while the three
27-dimensional cases are simple algebras of type \(B_{13}\).

## 5. Exact identification of the 606- and 799-dimensional images

E59 recomputes the exact rational commutant projectors for each of the four
32-dimensional sectors.  It verifies that they are pairwise orthogonal
idempotents, sum to the identity, and commute with both generators.  Rational
bases of their images concatenate to an invertible matrix which conjugates
both generators exactly to block diagonal form.

The pairwise exact Hom-space matrices are

\[
 \begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\0&1&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}
 \quad\text{for `001` and `111`,}\qquad
 \begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}
 \quad\text{for `011` and `101`.}
\]

Thus every constituent has scalar endomorphism ring over \(\mathbb Q\), and
distinct constituents have no intertwiners.  E59 also solves the exact
invariant-bilinear-form equations for each traceless constituent action.  Every
symmetric, alternating, or unsymmetrized invariant-form space is zero.  This
is an audit rather than an assumption used to label a classical type.

The decisive upper bound is even simpler.  In the exact component bases the
block trace vectors of \((A,B)\) are

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
 s&\operatorname{tr}_{\rm blocks}A&\operatorname{tr}_{\rm blocks}B\\ \hline
001&(0,0,0)&(2,-2,24)\\
111&(0,0,0)&(-2,2,-24)\\
011&(0,0)&(-6,14)\\
101&(0,0)&(6,-14).
\end{array}
\]

Every bracket has trace zero on every block.  Hence an image with constituent
dimensions \(d_1,\ldots,d_k\) lies in

\[
 E_t=\left\{\operatorname{diag}(X_1,\ldots,X_k):
 (\operatorname{tr}X_1,\ldots,\operatorname{tr}X_k)\in\mathbb Qt\right\},

\dim E_t=1+\sum_i(d_i^2-1).
\]

Thus the exact upper bounds are

\[
 1+2(4^2-1)+(24^2-1)=606,
 \qquad
 1+(4^2-1)+(28^2-1)=799.
\]

The same component-basis modular minors attain those bounds, so the rational
images equal \(E_t\).  Splitting off the block-scalar matrix

\[
 z=\operatorname{diag}\left(\frac{t_1}{d_1}I_{d_1},\ldots,
                            \frac{t_k}{d_k}I_{d_k}\right)
\]

gives the central line and the full traceless block algebras.  This proves the
\(A_3+A_3+A_{23}\) and \(A_3+A_{27}\) identifications over \(\mathbb Q\), not
merely modulo a prime.

## 6. Onsager-quotient corollary

**[EXTERNAL]** The Date--Roan closed-ideal classification and its source audit
are recorded in `proofs/onsager_quotient_nogo.md` and
`results/algebra_structure/oa_quotient.json`.  The theorem proved there is:
every finite-dimensional semisimple quotient of the complex Onsager algebra,
and therefore every Levi factor of an arbitrary finite-dimensional Onsager
quotient, is a direct sum of copies of \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\).  Its proof first
passes to the semisimple quotient, making the relevant kernel closed; it does
not incorrectly assume that every arbitrary kernel is closed.

Let \(\rho_{000}:\mathfrak g_{\mathbb Q}\to
\mathfrak{so}(F_{000},\mathbb Q)\) be the exact sector quotient just certified.
It is surjective by the dimension squeeze.  Scalar extension is right exact,
so

\[
 \rho_{000,\mathbb C}:\mathfrak g_{\mathbb C}\twoheadrightarrow
 \mathfrak{so}(42,\mathbb C)
\]

is a surjection onto a simple algebra of type \(D_{21}\), not
\(\mathfrak{sl}_2\).

If \(\mathfrak g_{\mathbb C}\) were an Onsager quotient, its further quotient
\(\mathfrak{so}(42,\mathbb C)\) would also be an Onsager quotient.  The audited
corollary forbids this.  Finally, any generating pair satisfying the normalized
Dolan--Grady relations would induce a surjection from the universal Onsager
presentation, giving the same contradiction.  This proves the unconditional
open-`2x4` no-go stated in Section 1.

## 7. Reproduction and limits

Run the dedicated standalone verifier only:

```bash
.venv/bin/python tests/test_levi_images_2x4.py
```

It independently rebuilds the `000` exact common-kernel quotient, recomputes
the one-dimensional exact symmetric-form space and all containment identities,
checks good reduction of the same basis, and independently regenerates the 861
literal words and their selected minor.  It also checks the three promoted
\(B_{13}\) records, the exact 606/799 identifications, the hash-bound audited
Onsager corollary, and every generated artifact check.

The experiment itself is:

```bash
.venv/bin/python experiments/e59_levi_images_2x4.py
```

Its artifact has the required `provenance/data/checks` envelope at
`results/algebra_structure/levi_images_2x4.json`; final stdout is `PASS` exactly
when all embedded checks pass.

**[UNRESOLVED]** This artifact does not identify the complete Levi direct sum of
the full 2952-dimensional open-`2x4` algebra.  Sector images can share simple
factors, so adding their dimensions is invalid without exact joint-linkage and
full-kernel certificates.  Nothing here determines a thermodynamic-limit free
energy, a critical coupling, or an exact solution of the three-dimensional
model.
