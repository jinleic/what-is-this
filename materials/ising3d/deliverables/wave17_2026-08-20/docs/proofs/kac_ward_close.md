# Exact thin-box closure of 14 Kac--Ward branches; branch `11111` remains unresolved

## Abstract

**[THEOREM]** Fourteen of the fifteen branches left unresolved by the wave-7 gauge-tree calculation are empty over `Q`. Together with the 17 imported wave-7 certificates, this gives 31 exact `EMPTY_OVER_Q` statuses among the 32 named branches. Every new decision is witnessed by a two-equation integer identity whose left side is a nonzero constant.

**[UNRESOLVED]** Branch `11111` survives. Exact elimination gives useful negative and structural information, but no emptiness certificate and no rational point: its systematic thin-box span has no constant certificate; its thin-plane ideal has an explicitly certified 16-dimensional prime radical; a degree-at-most-one rational Nullstellensatz search is exactly exhausted; and four finite-field points lead only to point-specific p-adic nonlift certificates. Therefore there is no global Kac--Ward no-go theorem here, and the three-dimensional model remains unsolved.

Artifacts: `experiments/e62_kw_close.py`, `results/kac_ward/close.json`, and `tests/test_kw_close.py`.

## 1. Necessary finite-box equations

For a free box `B` and an even order `k`, write

`F_(B,k)(U) = Tr_B(U^k) + 2 k [v^k] log P_B(v)`.

**[LEMMA]** A scalar translation-invariant Kac--Ward determinant identity on all free cubic boxes must have `F_(B,k)=0` on every such box and order. A box with one side equal to one is a free induced subgraph of the cubic lattice, so a universal finite-box identity must also hold on these thin boxes. No limiting or floating-point argument is involved.

The five gauge-tree entries are ordered as

`(+x->+y, +x->-y, +x->+z, +x->-z, +y->-x)`.

A branch bit is `0` when the corresponding entry vanishes and `1` when a nonzero entry is normalized to one. The experiment imports the 17 exact wave-7 certificates without changing them and attacks only the 15 unresolved labels.

## 2. Systematic exact thin-box scan

**[COMPUTATION]** The new scan uses all 27 coordinate permutations of the planar rectangles with canonical shapes

`2x2x1, 3x2x1, 4x2x1, 3x3x1, 4x3x1, 4x4x1`

at orders `k=4,6,8,10`. Thus 108 exact integer equations are tested. The target constants are regenerated by exact even-subgraph enumeration and exact formal logarithms; the trace side is generated by exact closed-word counting. Integer nullspaces of all nonconstant monomial columns are then checked for a vector with nonzero constant pairing.

Two branch-reduced identities result. For

`01010, 01011, 01100, 01101, 10011, 10101, 11010, 11011, 11100, 11101`,

one has exactly

`2 F_(2x1x2,8) - F_(2x1x3,8) = 16`.

For

`01110, 01111, 10111, 11110`,

one has exactly

`2 F_(2x2x1,8) - F_(2x3x1,8) = 16`.

The raw even-subgraph data used by both orientations are

- `P_(2x1x2)(v)=1+v^4`, with `[v^8] log P=-1/2` and required trace `8`;
- `P_(2x1x3)(v)=1+2v^4+v^6`, with `[v^8] log P=-2` and required trace `32`;
- the corresponding coordinate-permuted values for `2x2x1` and `2x3x1`.

The branch substitutions make all nonconstant trace monomials cancel. The remaining target contribution is `2(-8)-(-32)=16`.

The exact reduced differences fall into the two displayed coordinate-orientation classes. The experiment verifies polynomial equality separately on every branch and then reuses the corresponding raw identity; it does not infer an unverified gauge-tree orbit from geometric symmetry alone.

**[THEOREM]** At a common zero of the selected equations the left side of either displayed identity would be zero, contradicting `16 != 0`. Hence all fourteen listed branches are empty over `Q`. The JSON stores the coefficient terms, raw lattice constants, and the verified symbolic identity separately for every branch. The independent test regenerates each `P_B`, each logarithmic coefficient, each trace polynomial, and each branch substitution before checking the constant.

## 3. The exact thin-plane radical on branch `11111`

For each coordinate plane let `A` and `B` be its two oppositely oriented plaquette-turn monomials and let `s` be a selected opposite straight-weight product. Exact rational row reduction of the 27 equations from the nine smallest thin boxes gives, plane by plane,

`S=A+B+2`, `P=AB-1`, and `T=s(A+B)+2`

in the `Q`-linear span of the raw lattice constraints. All row coefficients are stored.

The following polynomial identities are immediate and are also checked exactly:

`A S - P = (A+1)^2`,

`B S - P = (B+1)^2`,

`(s S - T)/2 = s-1`.

**[LEMMA]** Over characteristic zero, every common zero therefore has `A=B=-1` and `s=1`. Across the three planes this gives six oriented turn-product relations and three straight-product relations.

The nine exponent rows have Smith diagonal

`(1,1,1,1,1,1,1,1,1)`.

Consequently their lattice is saturated. All 25 active coordinates occur in nonzero monomial relations, and the radical quotient is a domain parametrized by a 16-dimensional algebraic torus. This supplies an exact prime-radical reduction of the thin subsystem, rather than treating a sampled Jacobian as a decomposition.

**[UNRESOLVED]** Adding the genuinely three-dimensional equations to this prime radical is not decided by the reduction.

## 4. Exhausted constant and bounded-multiplier searches

For branch `11111`, the 108-equation thin-box scan has 267 nonconstant monomials. The nonconstant coefficient matrix and the matrix including the constant row both have exact `Q`-rank `45`; the nullspace dimension is `63`.

**[THEOREM]** No degree-zero rational Nullstellensatz certificate exists in this recorded order-through-ten thin-box span. This is a theorem only about the stated finite linear span.

A second search combines 42 exact constraints: the nine primary thin shapes and five three-dimensional boxes, all through `k=8`. Their exact polynomial span has rank 21. Choosing the recorded 21 raw equations as a basis and multiplying each by `1` and by each of the 25 variables gives one fixed integer Macaulay matrix with 546 columns.

For reductions of this same matrix at `p=101,1009,10007`, the column rank is 546 and adjoining the constant target gives rank 547 in every case. The matrix hash, basis indices, pivot hashes, and nonzero pivot products are stored.

**[THEOREM]** The corresponding characteristic-zero ranks are exactly 546 and 547: 546 and 547 are exact column-count upper bounds, while the good-prime minors give matching lower bounds for the same integer matrices. Therefore the constant is not in the rational Macaulay column span, and there is no rational Nullstellensatz certificate with multiplier degree at most one for this 42-constraint system.

**[UNRESOLVED]** This bounded negative result does not exclude higher-degree multipliers and does not prove that the ideal has a characteristic-zero zero.

## 5. Modular points, Jacobians, and selected p-adic paths

**[COMPUTATION]** Exact radical parametrization finds explicit common zeros of all 42 construction equations over `F_3`, `F_5`, `F_7`, and `F_11`. The stored Jacobian ranks are respectively `7,12,12,12`. These are ranks at verified points of the variety, not at arbitrary samples.

A deterministic singular Hensel section sets every free correction coordinate to zero. Exact linearized lifting gives:

- at `p=3`, the displayed point has no lift modulo `3^2`;
- at `p=5,7,11`, the displayed points lift through `p^2`, but those selected residues have no next digit modulo `p^3`.

For every blocked residue, the artifact stores a left-kernel vector `lambda` satisfying

`lambda J = 0 mod p`, while `lambda b != 0 mod p`,

where `b` is the exact next-digit right-hand side. These are independently checkable nonlift certificates for the displayed residues only.

The exact `3x3x3` holdout residuals at the last selected precision are

- `p=3`: `(0,0,0)` modulo `3`;
- `p=5`: `(0,0,24)` modulo `25`;
- `p=7`: `(0,0,31)` modulo `49`;
- `p=11`: `(0,0,104)` modulo `121`.

Thus the three longer selected paths fail the holdout at `k=8`; the `p=3` point passes only modulo three and does not lift along the displayed section. The least nonnegative integer representatives are also substituted over `Z` into all construction and holdout equations and are not rational solutions.

**[UNRESOLVED]** A nonlift at one point, or at finitely many primes, does not imply `Q`-emptiness. No p-adic path reached rational-reconstruction precision, so no rational candidate was produced. Any future candidate is required to pass all 42 exact construction equations and the exact `3x3x3` holdout before it may be reported.

## 6. Exact scope and conclusion

**[THEOREM]** The named gauge-tree ledger now contains 31 exact `EMPTY_OVER_Q` branches: 17 imported certificates and 14 new thin-box constant identities.

**[UNRESOLVED]** Branch `11111` remains open. Therefore no global no-go is claimed. Moreover, the 32 labels stratify the zero/nonzero patterns of one selected five-edge gauge tree; they are not an enumeration of all support patterns among all 30 transition weights. Nothing here solves the full translation-invariant scalar family, position-dependent or matrix-valued extensions, or the three-dimensional Ising model.
