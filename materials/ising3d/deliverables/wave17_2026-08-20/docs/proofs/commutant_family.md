# Joint commutants on open `2 x L` Ising ladders

## 1. Scope and exact conclusions

For the open two-row ladder with `L` columns, let

\[
 A_L=\sum_{v}X_v,\qquad
 B_L=\sum_{\{u,v\}\in E(2\times L)}Z_uZ_v,
\]

and put

\[
 \mathcal C_L=\{T:[T,A_L]=[T,B_L]=0\}.
\]

All vector spaces below are full matrix spaces; no Pauli-weight or locality
restriction is imposed.

**[THEOREM — finite exact table].** Exact rational elimination and exact
invariant projectors give

| `L` | sites | `dim C_L` | split type over `Q` | type over `C` | centre dimension |
|---:|---:|---:|---|---|---:|
| 2 | 4 | 27 | `M4(Q) + M2(Q)^2 + Q^3` | `M4(C) + M2(C)^2 + C^3` | 6 |
| 3 | 6 | 12 | `M2(Q) + Q^8` | `M2(C) + C^8` | 9 |
| 4 | 8 | 41 | `M5(Q) + M2(Q) + Q^12` | `M5(C) + M2(C) + C^12` | 14 |
| 5 | 10 | **20** | `M2(Q)^2 + Q^12` | `M2(C)^2 + C^12` | 14 |

Tensoring the displayed rational decompositions with `R` gives the analogous
split real types. In particular, no complex or quaternionic division factor is
hidden in the real calculation.

These are theorems only for `L=2,3,4,5`. The `L=6` calculation below contains
an exact lower bound and a modular upper-bound scout, not an exact commutant
dimension. No all-`L` formula is asserted.

The certificate is generated by `experiments/e60_commutant_family.py`, stored
in `results/integrability/commutant_family.json`, and independently rebuilt at
`L=3` and `L=5` by `tests/test_commutant_family.py`.

## 2. Symmetry-character Hom blocks

Let `R` be row reflection, `C` column reflection, and `P=prod_v X_v` global
spin flip. They commute with `A_L` and `B_L` and generate

\[
 H=\langle R,C,P\rangle\cong C_2^3.
\]

For `chi=(chi_R,chi_C,chi_P)`, write

\[
 V_\chi=\{v:Rv=(-1)^{\chi_R}v,\ Cv=(-1)^{\chi_C}v,
                 Pv=(-1)^{\chi_P}v\}.
\]

**[LEMMA — exact orbit basis].** If `r` represents an `H`-orbit of spin
configurations, then

\[
 s_{\chi,r}=\sum_{g\in H}(-1)^{\chi\cdot g}|gr\rangle
\]

is either zero or a character vector. Taking one nonzero normalized vector per
orbit gives a rational basis of `V_chi`. In that basis `B_L` is diagonal with
integer entries, while `A_L` has integer entries. The Gram matrix is the
positive diagonal matrix of orbit sizes, so both restrictions are Hermitian
for this exact rational inner product.

For two sectors, first impose `XB_source=B_target X`. It deletes every matrix
entry joining unequal `B` energies. On the remaining variables solve

\[
 X A_{\rm source}=A_{\rm target}X
\]

by an exact sparse `QQ` DomainMatrix rank/nullspace calculation, followed by
anchor normalization to `Fraction` vectors and exact substitution. Thus every entry of the table

\[
 h_{\chi\psi}=\dim_{\mathbb Q}
 \operatorname{Hom}_{(A,B)}(V_\psi,V_\chi)
\]

is an exact rational nullity. Orbit-metric adjunction identifies opposite Hom
blocks, so only the upper triangle has to be eliminated. The integer equations
also show that rational and complex Hom dimensions agree after scalar
extension.


## 3. Exact irreducible projectors and the Wedderburn calculation

A Hom table alone does not always determine the answer. For example, the
`000` diagonal block at `L=4` has dimension five and is noncommutative: it is
`M2(Q)+Q`, not `Q^5`. The certificate therefore constructs invariant
projectors rather than guessing a nonnegative Gram factorization.

**[LEMMA — rational minimal-projector certificate].** In each character
sector, let `D_chi=End_(A,B)(V_chi)` and let `E_1,...,E_d` be the canonical
rational nullspace basis. Orbit-metric adjunction is rational. The experiment
chooses a small exact self-adjoint combination of `E_j+E_j^dagger`, computes
its multiplication matrix in the `d`-dimensional regular representation, and
finds its distinct rational eigenvalues. For each eigenvalue `lambda`, it
forms

\[
 Q_\lambda=\prod_{\mu\ne\lambda}
 \frac{T-\mu I}{\lambda-\mu}.
\]

Every stored `Q_lambda` is checked exactly to be idempotent,
metric-self-adjoint, mutually orthogonal, and part of a resolution of the
identity. Most importantly, the complete rational commutant basis is
sandwiched and row-reduced to verify

\[
 \dim_{\mathbb Q} Q_\lambda D_\chi Q_\lambda=1.
\]

Therefore its range is irreducible after extension to `C`: a proper complex
reducing submodule would give a non-scalar element in this corner. This
argument also splits repeated copies inside one character sector.

For every pair of minimal projectors `Q` and `Q'`, all exact Hom-basis maps are
sandwiched as `Q'XQ`. Their span has dimension zero or one. A nonzero span
identifies two irreducible copies; its stored witness has full irreducible
rank. Connected components of this exact intertwiner graph are the isomorphism
classes. If a component contains `m` copies, the corresponding commutant block
is `M_m(Q)`. Rational adjoints normalize the nonzero witnesses to rational
matrix units, so all factors are split over `Q`; scalar extension gives the
real and complex tables in Section 1.

The non-scalar-multiplicity components are:

| `L` | multiplicity | irreducible module dimension | sector copies | mechanism |
|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| 2 | 4 | 1 | `000,010,100,110` | common `(A,B)=(0,0)` eigenlines |
| 2 | 2 | 1 | `011,101` | common `(-2,0)` eigenlines |
| 2 | 2 | 1 | `011,101` | common `(2,0)` eigenlines |
| 3 | 2 | 6 | `100,110` | exact higher-dimensional module equivalence |
| 4 | 5 | 1 | two copies in `000`, one in each of `010,100,110` | common `(0,0)` eigenspace |
| 4 | 2 | 27 | `100,110` | exact higher-dimensional module equivalence |
| 5 | 2 | 10 | `000,010` | exact higher-dimensional module equivalence |
| 5 | 2 | 120 | `100,110` | exact higher-dimensional module equivalence |

All remaining components have multiplicity one. Squaring the listed
multiplicities and adding one per remaining component gives exactly the four
Hom-table sums.

## 4. Why `2 x 3` is `M2(C) + C^8`

**[THEOREM — structural explanation of the twelve dimensions].** At `L=3`,
the sectors `000,010,011,111` are irreducible. The `001` sector splits into a
common eigenline with `(A,B)=(0,-1)` and its nine-dimensional irreducible
orthogonal complement. The `101` sector similarly splits into a line with
`(A,B)=(0,1)` and a nine-dimensional complement. These four constituents are
pairwise inequivalent to everything outside their own sector.

The only off-character Hom spaces are

\[
 \operatorname{Hom}(V_{100},V_{110})\cong\mathbb Q,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Hom}(V_{110},V_{100})\cong\mathbb Q.
\]

Their exact rank-six witnesses identify the two six-dimensional irreducible
modules. Thus there is one multiplicity-two class and eight multiplicity-one
classes:

\[
 \boxed{\mathcal C_3\cong M_2(\mathbb Q)\oplus\mathbb Q^8},
 \qquad
 \mathcal C_3\otimes_{\mathbb Q}\mathbb C
 \cong M_2(\mathbb C)\oplus\mathbb C^8.
\]

This also separates the four dimensions beyond the geometric `C2^3` algebra:
two directions split the `001` and `101` character identities, while the two
off-diagonal rank-six intertwiners complete the `M2` block.

## 5. The new exact `2 x 5` theorem

**[THEOREM].** On the open `2 x 5` ladder,

\[
 \dim_{\mathbb Q}\mathcal C_5=20,
 \qquad
 \mathcal C_5\cong M_2(\mathbb Q)^2\oplus\mathbb Q^{12}.
\]

The first repeated irreducible has dimension ten and occurs once in each of
`000` and `010`. The second has dimension 120 and occurs once in each of `100`
and `110`. Every other exact minimal projector gives an inequivalent
multiplicity-one constituent. Consequently

\[
 \mathcal C_5\otimes\mathbb C
 \cong M_2(\mathbb C)^2\oplus\mathbb C^{12},
 \qquad \dim Z(\mathcal C_5)=14.
\]

This is not an inference from modular agreement. Every one of the 36 upper
triangle Hom blocks was row-reduced over `Q`, every canonical nullspace was
substituted in the integer equations, and all rational projector corners and
projected Hom spaces were checked exactly. The independent test uses a
different orbit normalization, reversed variables/equations, and
greatest-column sparse elimination at a second large prime to reproduce the
whole `H_5` table independently. Its modular ranks are rigorous upper bounds
on rational Hom dimensions and match the stored exact-Q bases, closing both
sides of each equality.

## 6. A real finite mechanism: common zero modes

The ladder is bipartite. Let `U_X` be the product of `X` on one checkerboard
sublattice and let `U_Z=prod_v Z_v`. Direct Pauli conjugation proves, for every
`L`,

\[
 U_XA_LU_X=A_L,\quad U_XB_LU_X=-B_L,\qquad
 U_ZA_LU_Z=-A_L,\quad U_ZB_LU_Z=B_L.
\]

**[LEMMA — all-size spectral dualities, not charges].** Row reflection swaps
the two checkerboard sublattices, so `R U_X R=P U_X`. Column reflection does
the same for even `L` and fixes the sublattices for odd `L`. These identities
rigorously organize some opposite-eigenvalue and character pairings in the
finite tables. Neither `U_X` nor `U_Z` is in the joint commutant on the full
space: each changes the sign of one generator. They both preserve the common
zero space below, where the sign change disappears. Thus bipartiteness
explains a mechanism but does not supply an extensive conserved charge.

If `W_L=ker A_L intersect ker B_L`, Hermiticity makes `W_L` a reducing
subspace and both generators vanish on it. Hence arbitrary endomorphisms of
`W_L`, extended by zero on its orthogonal complement, commute with both:

\[
 \operatorname{End}(W_L)\subseteq\mathcal C_L.
\]

This explains the large matrix blocks at even sizes; they are not mysterious
individual charges. Exact rational kernels in the eight characters give

| `L` | `dim(W_L intersect V_chi)` in character order | `dim W_L` | resulting block |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 2 | `1,0,1,0,1,0,1,0` | 4 | `M4` |
| 3 | all zero | 0 | none |
| 4 | `2,0,1,0,1,0,1,0` | 5 | `M5` |
| 5 | all zero | 0 | none |
| 6 | `1,0,2,0,2,0,2,0` | **7** | at least `M7` |

The absence for odd `L` at eigenvalue zero also follows immediately because
`B_L` has `3L-2` bond terms, an odd number when `L` is odd, and therefore no
zero computational-basis eigenvalue.

**[THEOREM — exact `L=6` lower bound].** At `L=6`, exact rational elimination
gives `dim W_6=7`, so `End(W_6)` has dimension 49. Every one of the eight
character sectors has a nonzero complement to `W_6`. Therefore no nonzero
linear combination of the eight character projectors is supported only on
`W_6`, and

\[
 \dim_{\mathbb Q}\mathcal C_6\ge 49+8=\boxed{57}.
\]

This already refutes the even two-point linear extrapolation `7L+13`, which
fits `L=2,4` but predicts only 55 at `L=6`.


**[COMPUTATION — modular scout, not an equality over `Q`].** At
`p=1000000007`, exact finite-field elimination of every `L=6` character Hom
block gives total nullity **71**. Therefore the certified characteristic-zero
interval is

\[
 \boxed{57\le \dim_{\mathbb Q}\mathcal C_6\le 71}.
\]

The prime-field nullity is a rigorous upper bound on the rational nullity
because `rank_Fp <= rank_Q` for the same integer matrix. The complete modular
Hom table is recorded in the JSON. This one-prime result is not promoted to
an exact rational dimension or a Wedderburn type.

## 7. Geometric symmetries versus module symmetries

The eight character projectors span the represented group algebra of row
reflection, column reflection, and global spin flip in every exact case. The
full separation is

| `L` | full dimension | baseline `C2^3` image | full spatial-permutation image | beyond all spatial permutations |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | 27 | 8 | 13 | 14 |
| 3 | 12 | 8 | 8 | 4 |
| 4 | 41 | 8 | 8 | 33 |
| 5 | 20 | 8 | 8 | 12 |

For the square, the graph automorphism group is `D4`; its eight site
automorphisms times spin flip give sixteen permutation operators, whose exact
represented group-algebra rank is thirteen. For `L>=3`, the ladder graph
automorphism group is generated by the two reflections, so including spin
flip gives exactly the baseline eight-dimensional image.

**[LEMMA — all configuration-permutation symmetries].** Any permutation of
computational basis states commuting with `A_L` is an automorphism of the
`2L`-cube, hence a bit translation followed by a site-coordinate permutation.
Preserving `B_L` for every configuration forces the coordinate permutation to
preserve the ladder edge set. It also forces the translation bits to agree
across every edge; connectedness makes the translation either the identity or
global bit flip. Thus the preceding graph automorphisms and spin flip exhaust
configuration-permutation symmetries.

Consequently, at `L>=3` every dimension beyond eight is genuinely
non-monomial. The exact projector/intertwiner construction classifies it as a
module symmetry: splitting projectors, equivalences between distinct
characters, and, at `L=4`, two intra-character matrix-unit directions between
the repeated `000` copies.

**[UNRESOLVED — locality and integrability].** A finite full-matrix module
symmetry is not automatically an extensive local charge. No compatible
bounded-range density, translation-sum recurrence, or induction in `L` is
certified here. In fact, the exact types change sharply with `L`. These finite
commutants therefore do not establish integrability and say nothing about an
exact solution of the three-dimensional Ising model.

## 8. Formula audit

Two natural small-size extrapolations fail:

1. The `L=3` claim `M2(C)+C^8` for all odd `L` has minimal tested
   counterexample `L=5`, where the exact answer is `M2(C)^2+C^12`.
2. The two-point parity-linear fit gives `4L` at odd `L=3,5` and `7L+13` at
   even `L=2,4`. Its even branch has minimal tested counterexample `L=6`:
   the exact lower bound 57 already exceeds the predicted 55.

The odd values `12,20` happen to fit `4L`, but two data points and no local
recurrence do not constitute a theorem. The all-`L` dimension, module
multiplicities, and Wedderburn type remain **[UNRESOLVED]**.
