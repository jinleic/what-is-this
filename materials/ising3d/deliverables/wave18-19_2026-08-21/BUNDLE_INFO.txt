ISING3D EXACT-RESEARCH BUNDLE — WAVES 18 AND 19
================================================

Purpose
-------
This directory holds the unified research paper and the exact artifacts behind the waves-18/19
results. The 3D Ising model remains UNSOLVED. Nothing here derives a thermodynamic-limit free
energy, magnetisation, correlation function, spectrum, critical coupling, or critical exponent.

Headline results in this bundle
-------------------------------
1. All-L classification of the open 2xL local-term Lie algebra, for EVERY L >= 2:
   so_m(Q)+so_m(Q) for even L, sp_m(Q)+sp_m(Q) for odd L, m = 2^(n-1), n = 2L,
   dim = 2^(n-1)(2^(n-1)-(-1)^L). Closes the saturation step open since wave 16.
2. An exact TRICHOTOMY for arbitrary layer graphs. For bipartite Gamma with a Hamiltonian path:
   path -> n(2n-1); even cycle -> 2n(2n-1); Delta >= 3 -> 2^(2n-2) - (-1)^(n/2) 2^(n-1) for even n
   and 2^(2n-2) - 1 for odd n, depending only on n. Non-bipartite: odd cycle -> 2n(2n-1), otherwise
   the full noncentral even algebra 2^(2n-1) - 2. The two low branches are exactly the
   free-fermion-solvable geometries (open chain, ring) and are quadratic in n; branching is
   exponential. The jump occurs at the same Delta >= 3 threshold as the Dolan-Grady defect, the claw
   obstruction, and the tridiagonal no-go.
3. An all-L free-fermion no-go for the ladder TRANSFER generators, every L >= 3, assembled with a
   contiguous seam table and an unconditional partition-family theorem for L >= 32.
4. Theorem U2 restored after four waves: exact characteristic-zero r(T) = 417, r_all(T) = 445.
5. Kac-Ward: no orbit quotient exists (16384 singleton orbits) and the complete F_5-unit census over
   4^13 = 67108864 points kills every local point. Branch 11111 nonetheless remains OPEN over Q.
6. Exactly refuted routes, recorded so they are not retried: localizing isotropic decoupling, group
   rigidity for the isotropic spectral question, ad_A Vandermonde grade separation, a constant-c
   bound W_L <= (1-c)K_L, and a universal two-generator threshold over all branching bipartite
   graphs (K_{2,3} gives 44 < 45, K_{3,3} gives 63 < 66).

Verification
------------
Every front's standalone verifier was re-run by the lead in the main checkout after delivery, not
only by the agent that produced it. All printed PASS. The lead additionally re-derived, from
scratch and independently of any producer, the 2x6 razor arithmetic, the whole pair-product table,
every trichotomy and cycle dimension, the W_L closed form against all seven certified values, and
the Q(L) threshold crossover — 45 arithmetic checks, all passing.

One correction found by that process is recorded rather than hidden: the lead initially read the
ladder index set as ordinary partitions and computed a crossover at L = 41, which would have left a
gap at L = 32..40. The producing front resolved it decisively with machine-verified family ranks
(21, 29, 41 at L = 8, 9, 10) showing the index set counts zero entries, equivalently partitions with
all parts >= 2, whose crossover is exactly L = 32. Two artifact checks now pin both readings.

Provenance and limitations
--------------------------
- K_c = 0.221654626 is comparison-only; it was never used to select, fit, tune, or validate.
- Modular ranks and gcd degrees are used only in their proven one-sided direction.
- Finite series do not prove a closed form, D-finiteness, or a thermodynamic-limit solution.
- Algebra results concern finite graphs and the algebra generated by the stated generators; they are
  not statements about transfer spectra or about solvability of the 3D model.
- The staged docs are a snapshot; executable reproduction should use the repository root.
- MANIFEST.json records byte sizes and SHA-256 for every staged file except MANIFEST.json itself.
