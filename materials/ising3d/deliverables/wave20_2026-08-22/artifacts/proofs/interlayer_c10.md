## 1. Connected-correlation notation

[LEMMA] Let $n=2m$, let $[n]=\{1,\ldots,n\}$ be labelled in-plane
slots, and anchor $x_1=0$. For an even slot set $S$, define the
connected layer correlation
\[
 C_S=\kappa_{\langle\cdot\rangle_2}\!\left(\sigma_{x_i}:i\in S\right).
\]
Thus $C_2=G$, $C_4=U$, $C_6=W$, and the next two objects are
denoted $C_8=W8$ and $C_{10}=W10$.

[LEMMA] Write $\operatorname{EP}(S)$ for the set partitions of $S$
whose blocks all have even size, and put
\[
 E(S)=\sum_{\rho\in\operatorname{EP}(S)}\prod_{D\in\rho}C_D,
 \qquad E(\varnothing)=1.
\]
Zero-field spin-flip symmetry kills every odd connected cumulant, so the
ordinary moment-cumulant identity gives
\[
 E(S)=\left\langle\prod_{i\in S}\sigma_{x_i}\right\rangle_2.
\]
This remains exact when physical positions coincide: slots stay labelled and
the joint-cumulant identity is applied to repeated random variables.

[LEMMA] For a composition $a=(a_1,\ldots,a_k)\models m$, split $[2m]$
into consecutive gap-slot sets $I_0,\ldots,I_{k-1}$ with
$|I_j|=2a_{j+1}$. Let $\Pi_a$ contain the Bell partitions $\pi$ of
$[2m]$ such that
\[
 |B\cap I_j|\equiv0\pmod2
 \quad\text{for every }B\in\pi\text{ and every }j.
\]
For a block $B$ and layer $\ell\in\{0,\ldots,k\}$ set
\[
 S_{\ell,B}=B\cap(I_{\ell-1}\cup I_\ell),
 \qquad I_{-1}=I_k=\varnothing,
\]
and let $\mu(\pi)=(-1)^{|\pi|-1}(|\pi|-1)!$.

## 2. Closed all-$v$ formula for $c_8$

[THEOREM] Define
\[
 \mathcal K_a(x_1,\ldots,x_8)=
 \sum_{\pi\in\Pi_a}\mu(\pi)
 \prod_{B\in\pi}\prod_{\substack{0\le\ell\le k\\S_{\ell,B}\ne\varnothing}}
 E(S_{\ell,B}).
 \tag{1}
\]
Then the direct-coupling eighth interlayer coefficient is the exact
coefficientwise formal identity
\[
 \boxed{
 c_{8,q}(v)=
 \sum_{a\models4}\frac{1}{\prod_j(2a_j)!}
 \sum_{x_2,\ldots,x_8\in\mathbb Z^2}^{\prime}
 \mathcal K_a(0,x_2,\ldots,x_8).}
 \tag{2}
\]
Equation (2) is valid to every formal order in $v$; it is not a truncation
through $v^{12}$.

[LEMMA] Formula (2) follows by expanding the eighth cumulant of the vertical
bond sum over Bell partitions, factorizing each Bell-block moment over
independent layers, and applying the layer moment-cumulant identity $M=E$.
Block parity leaves precisely $\Pi_a$. The number of ordered assignments of
the gap multiset is $8!/\prod_j(2a_j)!$; division by the Taylor factor
$8!$ gives the coefficient in (2).

## 3. The forced $W8$ sector

[THEOREM] Put $R_8=E([8])-W8_{[8]}$. The complete part of (2) containing
at least one $W8$ is
\[
 \frac{W8_{[8]}^2+2W8_{[8]}R_8}{8!}
 +\sum_{a=1}^{3}
 \frac{E(I_a)W8_{[8]}E(J_a)}{(2a)!(8-2a)!},
 \tag{3}
\]
where in the summand $I_a=\{1,\ldots,2a\}$ and
$J_a=[8]\setminus I_a$ represent the two-gap composition $(a,4-a)$.
No profile with three or four occupied gaps contains $W8$.

[LEMMA] A $W8_{[8]}$ atom can arise only from an induced layer moment
whose slot set is all of $[8]$. That forces the Bell block to contain every
slot, so only the one-block Bell partition contributes to (3), with Möbius
coefficient one. The single-gap profile has two such layers; a two-gap profile
has only its middle layer; a longer profile has none.

[THEOREM] In the universal polynomial ring generated by the connected layer
symbols, the coefficient of $W8_{[8]}^2$ in $c_{8,q}$ is exactly
$1/8!=1/40320$. It cannot be cancelled and does not belong to the subring
generated by $G,U,W$. Therefore the partition-cumulant method cannot close
$c_8$ with the previously solved two-, four-, and six-point connected
objects alone.

[THEOREM] An exact algebraic counterexample to a lower-object closure is given
by two evaluations that agree on every $G,U,W$ symbol: set all lower symbols
to zero in both evaluations, set $W8_{[8]}=0$ in the first and
$W8_{[8]}=1$ in the second, and set all other $W8$ symbols to zero. Any
polynomial in $G,U,W$ has the same value under both evaluations, while the
single-gap eighth-order polynomial differs by $1/40320$.

## 5. The forced $W10$ sector and the all-order obstruction

[THEOREM] Put $R_{10}=E([10])-W10_{[10]}$. The complete $W10$ sector is
\[
 \frac{W10_{[10]}^2+2W10_{[10]}R_{10}}{10!}
 +\sum_{a=1}^{4}
 \frac{E(I_a)W10_{[10]}E(J_a)}{(2a)!(10-2a)!}.
 \tag{4}
\]
Only $(5),(4,1),(3,2),(2,3),(1,4)$ support $W10$. The numbers
of even set partitions at sizes $2,4,6,8,10$ are respectively
$1,4,31,379,6556$, which gives the $W10$-sector counts in the table.

[THEOREM] For every $m\ge1$, the universal connected-correlation
polynomial for $c_{2m,q}$ contains
\[
 \boxed{\frac{1}{(2m)!}C_{[2m]}^2.}
 \tag{5}
\]
The term is unique: two layers can both carry all $2m$ slots only in the
single-gap composition $(m)$; a $C_{[2m]}$ atom forces the one-block Bell
partition; its Möbius coefficient is one and its Taylor placement is
$1/(2m)!$.

[THEOREM] Equation (5) proves a strict hierarchy for the universal
partition-cumulant method: no polynomial in connected layer objects of ranks
strictly below $2m$ can equal $c_{2m,q}$. In particular, $W10$ is the
first irreducible connected datum at tenth interlayer order, just as $W8$
is forced at eighth order.

[THEOREM] The exact counterexample for $c_{10}$ is the analogous pair of
universal evaluations: keep all symbols of rank at most eight identical (for
example zero), but set $W10_{[10]}$ to zero versus one. Every proposed
lower-object polynomial agrees on the pair, whereas the one-gap tenth-order
polynomial differs by $1/10!=1/3628800$.

The spin-density-of-states route independently overlaps through $v^6$.

[LEMMA] Exact formal composition gives

 [w^{10}]q^4=\frac{1436}{945},\quad
 [w^{10}]q^6=\frac{43}{15},\quad
 [w^{10}]q^8=\frac83,\quad

\]

[THEOREM] The decoupled column $\log\cosh q$ and the all-order ladder

\[

 [v^2]c_{10,q}=\frac4{14175},

\[
 [v^0]c_{10,w}^{\mathrm{tot}}=\frac1{10},\qquad
 [v^0]c_{10,w}^{\mathrm{res}}=0,\qquad
 [v^2]c_{10,w}^{\mathrm{res}}=2.
\]

[UNRESOLVED] No $c_{10}$ finite-lattice coefficients from $v^4$ through

## 8. Scope and non-results

[THEOREM] The obstruction is a theorem in the universal polynomial ring of
connected layer cumulants. It proves exactly what a derivation using only the
partition-cumulant algebra can and cannot eliminate.

[UNRESOLVED] The algebraic counterexamples above are formal specializations,
not physical square-lattice Gibbs states. They do not prove that $W8$ or
$W10$ is algebraically independent after specialization to the 2D Ising
model, and they do not rule out model-specific Pfaffian, determinant,
non-polynomial, or auxiliary-data evaluations.

[UNRESOLVED] The anchored infinite-lattice sums are interpreted
coefficientwise as formal high-temperature series. No absolute-convergence
statement, convergence radius, analytic continuation, sign theorem, or
critical-point evaluation is proved.

[UNRESOLVED] The benchmark $K_c=0.221654626$ was not used to select, fit, or
check any formula. This work does not solve the three-dimensional Ising model.
