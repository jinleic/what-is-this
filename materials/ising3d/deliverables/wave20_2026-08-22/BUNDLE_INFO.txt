Scope
-----
This bundle contains the unified 26-page paper and the exact artifacts produced in wave 20.
The three-dimensional Ising model remains UNSOLVED. No thermodynamic-limit free energy,
magnetisation, correlation function, exact critical point, or exact exponent is claimed.

Provenance and limitations
--------------------------
- Exact combinatorial objects use integer/rational arithmetic. Modular ranks are used only in their
  rigorous one-sided direction; equal ranks at two primes are cross-checks, not rational equality.
- K_c=0.221654626 is comparison-only and was not used to select, fit, tune, or stop a derivation.
- MANIFEST.json records byte size and SHA-256 for every staged file except itself.
- Bundles are snapshots. Executable reproduction should use the repository root, not this copy.
