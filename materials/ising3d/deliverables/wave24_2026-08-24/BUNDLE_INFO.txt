Headline results
----------------
1. For every connected simple nonpath graph with n vertices and m edges, the physical
   full-spectrum exceptional set has at most [2^n(2n+2m)]^(n+2) points; uniformly at
   fixed n, at most [2^n(n^2+n)]^(n+2). Paths are excluded. This is an explicit
   algebraic size bound, not emptiness or a root list.
2. For every even Ising star and every nonempty log-Walsh sector of size 2r, the
   coefficient has strict sign (-1)^(r+1) for K!=0. At 0<v=tanh(K)<1, the
   degree-six weight is a pure-even matchgate. Its ordinary CP rank and every
   flattening rank are two, but its Walsh-basis nonnegative all-leg CP rank is
   exactly 32. The normalized checkerboard contraction is the positive cycle-space
   polynomial; no global nonplanar Pfaffian theorem follows.
3. For every even local degree d and 0<v=tanh(K)<1, the fixed contact-free
   selected-pivot/far-mark proper-even/odd Callen leakage matrix
   has rank 2^(d-1)-C(d,d/2)/2, and every leakage-canceling combination has zero
   pair coefficient. Cubic inversion leaves six exact higher-template relations in
   a 19x16 rank-13 block; deleting the pair row lowers rank to 12.
