# Exact 3D Ising research program

This repository is an evidence-first attempt to understand the nearest-neighbour ferromagnetic Ising model on the simple-cubic lattice.  It combines exact finite-volume arithmetic, transfer matrices, finite-lattice series, rigorous bounds, and algebraic integrability tests.  Its purpose is to prove or falsify sharply stated routes to a solution—not to label a fitted formula “exact.”

**Current status: the three-dimensional Ising model is not solved here.**  The repository contains exact finite results, rigorous bounds, verified controls, falsifications of published and structural proposals, and scoped no-go results for several standard mechanisms.

## Conventions

The fixed conventions are in [`problem_specification.md`](problem_specification.md) and [`notes/CODE_API.md`](notes/CODE_API.md):

- `K = beta J`, `v = tanh K`, `x = exp(-2K)`, `u = x^2`.
- `Z = exp(K n_b) sum_q c[q] x^q = 2^N (cosh K)^(n_b) P(v)`.
- A periodic direction of length 1 contributes no self-bond; length 2 contributes two parallel bonds.
- Combinatorial work uses exact integers or rational numbers.  Transcendental calculations use `mpmath` with recorded precision.

## What is **not** solved

- No exact thermodynamic-limit 3D free energy, partition function, spontaneous magnetisation, correlation function, or spectrum has been derived.
- `K_c` is bounded rigorously by the interval above but not known exactly. The short-series estimate `0.22294 +/- 0.00504` is numerical and is not an exact value.
- No exact 3D critical exponents or amplitude ratios are derived.  The CFT/RG values are external numerical constraints.
- Finite series through `v^28`/`x^56` do not establish a closed form or D-finiteness.  The completed negative frontiers (algebraicity, first/second-order differential algebraicity, linear ODE, Mahler) bound structure searches; they are not non-existence proofs for the true function.
- The Lee--Yang theorem is verified on finite lattices; the calibrated estimator is provably non-identifying at current cube sizes, so no 3D edge exponent is reported.
- The free-fermion, Dolan--Grady, tridiagonal, Kac--Ward, flux, local-charge, and tetrahedron results close only their stated ansatz classes.  They do not prove that no exact solution exists by another representation.
- Algebra dimensions and exponential-looking depth profiles are finite computations.  Exponential growth for the full infinite ladder/grid family remains unproved: `D_(2L) >= 2^L` is certified only for `L <= 9`; erase-rung, AB/BB, stationary two-letter, and six tested period-two one/three-letter induction mechanisms are obstructed, but other recursions remain possible.
- The deformation search does not cover simultaneous unrestricted nonlinear changes of both generators, non-invariant coefficients, or larger supports.
- The 3D Kac--Ward question is not fully closed: the positive-real cone and 31/32 rational gauge branches are excluded.  Branch `11111` has a nonsingular `Q_5` construction component, so ideal-emptiness/Nullstellensatz routes cannot close it; that local component fails an independent holdout, but the full branch over `Q`/`C` remains unresolved.
