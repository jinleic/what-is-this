   entire physical continuum to three exact algebraic candidates. All three are genuine

   c2>0, c4<0, c6>0, and proves visible pairwise non-closure.

   moments exactly, but remains exponential and supplies no thermodynamic compression.

   raw singleton-peeling induction while exact determinants close the finite rows.
6. Uniform phase-free graph-H1 twist averaging fails at girth on every cyclic simple graph.

7. Open boxes satisfy gamma >= max(0,ceil((abc-ab-bc-ca+4)/4)) and are planar exactly for
   side-one slabs and 2x2xL tubes. This is not an arbitrary Pfaffian-count lower bound.
8. The compact finite-memory SAW verifier now asserts both ordered content digests; memory
   14 remains unlaunched and no endpoint moves.
