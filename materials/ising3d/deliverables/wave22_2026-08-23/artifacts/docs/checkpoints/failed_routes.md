# Failed and bounded routes

This ledger distinguishes a decisive falsification from a scoped obstruction or an inconclusive resource wall.  “Failed” never means “there can be no exact solution”; it means the stated route did not satisfy its own necessary prediction.

## Published/formula routes

### Z.-D. Zhang (2007) four-fold integral — falsified

- **Route:** claimed exact simple-cubic free energy and critical condition.
- **Decisive checks:** the reproduced critical root is `0.2406059125298017...`, not `0.221654626(5)`; the claimed low-temperature magnetisation coefficient at `u^6` is `-18`, while exact enumeration gives `+14`.
- **Learned:** neither a complicated integral nor an asserted critical condition substitutes for exact finite/series coefficients.
- **Evidence:** `notes/falsification.md`, `notes/lit_claimed_solutions.md`, `results/falsification/falsification.json`.

### Degang Zhang (2021) critical line/free energy — falsified

- **Route:** operator-algebra/screw-boundary critical line and closed free energy.
- **Decisive checks:** permuting an anisotropic coupling triple yields three incompatible critical roots; the HT expansion first fails at `K^4`, predicting `-3` instead of exact `+3`.
- **Learned:** cubic rotation invariance and the first plaquette coefficient are cheap, benchmark-independent exact checks.
- **Evidence:** `notes/falsification.md`, `results/falsification/falsification.json`.

### CFT/RG compatibility of the claimed free energies — falsified

- **Route:** even if a formula fits some coefficients, its singularity might have the physical 3D exponent.
- **Decisive check:** after passing exact 2D-logarithmic and synthetic-power controls, the curvature classifier identifies both evaluable candidate free energies as logarithmic (`alpha=0`), not `alpha=0.110(1)`.
- **Scope:** Rosengren is not classified because the source supplies no complete callable free energy.
- **Evidence:** `notes/cft_constraints.md`, `results/cft/cft_constraints.json`.

### Rosengren/unknown-weight transform — unresolved, not reproduced

- **Route:** treat an integral transform with unspecified weight functions as an exact solution.
- **Blocker:** no independent rule determines the functions; fitting known series coefficients is interpolation rather than prediction.
- **Learned:** this route becomes testable only when a complete source-defined callable predicts coefficients not used to choose its weights.
- **Evidence:** `notes/lit_claimed_solutions.md`, `notes/literature_matrix.md`, `results/reference_data/claimed_solutions.json`.

## Duality route

### “3D Ising is self-dual” — falsified

- **Route:** use the scalar map `sinh(2K)sinh(2K*)=1` as in 2D and identify its fixed point with the 3D Ising critical point.
- **Decisive check:** exact finite-volume homology shows that the dual is a `Z_2` gauge model, with required twist/surface sectors and a different set of degrees of freedom.
- **Learned:** the coupling relation survives, but self-duality does not; naive single-sector torus identities also omit essential topological sectors.
- **Evidence:** `proofs/duality_3d.md`, `results/duality/duality.json`.

## Free-fermion and Onsager-algebra routes

### Term-wise Majorana bilinearisation on a genuine 2D layer — ruled out

- **Route:** represent every natural generator term `X_i` and `Z_iZ_j` by a distinct Majorana bilinear, as in a 1D Ising layer.
- **Obstruction:** a layer graph of maximum degree at least 3 has the forbidden anticommutation/claw pattern.  The construction exists exactly when maximum degree is at most 2.
- **Scope:** this rules out the stated injective term-wise map, not every nonlinear or exponentially enlarged fermionic representation.
- **Evidence:** `proofs/algebraic_obstruction.md`.

### Subexponential global Gaussian representation — ruled out

- **Route:** represent the full relevant even-spin Pauli algebra by quadratic Majorana operators with few modes.
- **Obstruction:** dimension comparison requires at least `2^(n-1)` Majorana modes.
- **Scope:** Onsager's 2D solution uses the much smaller algebra generated by the two sums, so this theorem does not itself decide the two-generator route.
- **Evidence:** `proofs/algebraic_obstruction.md`.

### Natural Dolan--Grady pair in 3D — fails exactly

- **Route:** use `A=sum X`, `B=sum ZZ` with the 2D Dolan--Grady normalization.
- **Decisive theorem:** DG1 always holds; DG2 has a nonzero degree-linear/quartic residual on every graph with a vertex of degree at least 3.  A square-grid layer has four quartic terms per bulk site.
- **Learned:** the failure is local and exactly countable by `sum_v C(deg(v),3)`.
- **Evidence:** `proofs/dolan_grady_defect.md`, `results/dg_theorem_check.json`.

### Historical corollary “DG iff 2-regular” — falsified and corrected

- **Counterexample:** `C3` plus an isolated vertex is not 2-regular but has zero residual.
- **Correct statement:** DG2 vanishes iff every degree is in `{0,2}`.  For physically connected nontrivial layer graphs this reduces to 2-regularity.
- **Learned:** disconnected and degree-zero edge cases must be checked whenever a theorem says “any graph.”
- **Evidence:** `proofs/dolan_grady_defect.md`, `results/audit/audit_replication.json`.

### Degree-dependent all-neighbour body order — falsified

- **Route:** infer that a degree-`d` vertex produces a `(d+1)`-body all-neighbour Dolan--Grady term.
- **Counterexample:** exact stars show that the nonlinear support remains a sum over neighbor triples for every `d`; only its multiplicity grows.
- **Evidence:** `experiments/e15_dg_local_structure.py`, `proofs/dolan_grady_defect.md`.

### “Induced claw iff exponential two-generator algebra” — falsified

- **Route:** promote the local claw obstruction to a global algebra-dimension criterion.
- **Counterexamples:** clawed `K_(1,3)`, `K_(1,4)`, and `K_4` have two-sum dimensions `24`, `33`, and `15`.
- **Learned:** a claw proves failure of the term-wise bilinear/DG mechanism; it does not determine global `dim<A,B>`.
- **Evidence:** `results/minimal_obstruction.json`, `proofs/dolan_grady_defect.md`.

### Exponential ladder algebra from finite fits — unproved

- **Route:** infer an all-size exponential theorem from rapid finite dimensions or the descriptive base `16.3818...`.
- **Evidence for:** exact finite certificate `D_(2L)>=2^L` for `L=2..6`; dimensions `11,263,2952` and lower bound `7183` on `2x5` at depth 20.
- **Why not closed:** no independent-word induction exists, and larger cases hit time/resource walls before saturation.
- **Learned:** modular finite ranks are rigorous lower bounds at those sizes, not asymptotics.
- **Evidence:** `notes/algebra_growth.md`, `results/algebra_growth/profiles.json`.

## Deformation and generalized Onsager routes

### F3 cancellation as a full integrable pair — falsified

- **Route:** cancel the quartic/complete DG2 residual by a symmetry-invariant local deformation of `A`.
- **Positive result:** an exact rational F3 vector cancels DG2 on the `3x3` torus.
- **Decisive failure:** the same pair has a DG1 coefficient `3` where the corresponding `ad_A(B)` coefficient is `0`, so no floating normalization can fix it.
- **Learned:** cancellation of the known quartic defect is possible but not sufficient for a Dolan--Grady algebra.
- **Evidence:** `proofs/dg_deformation_nogo.md`, `results/deformation/dg_deformation_search.json`.

### B-only invariant/weighted-edge deformations — ruled out in bounded families

- **Route:** restore DG relations by changing only edge weights or selected invariant local B orbits.
- **Outcome:** the torus B-linearized quartic system has no solution; full invariant F1 yields only decoupled on-site fields or zero; tested F4 and weighted-edge systems have certified empty/trivial solution sets.
- **Scope:** simultaneous nonlinear A+B deformations, non-invariant coefficients, and support beyond F4 remain open.
- **Evidence:** `proofs/dg_deformation_nogo.md`, `results/deformation/dg_deformation_search.json`.

### Natural tridiagonal/Askey--Wilson relation — ruled out on tested layers

- **Route:** relax Dolan--Grady to the full affine tridiagonal-pair parameter family.
- **Outcome:** exact rational systems have no solution on tested 3D torus layers through `5x5` and selected open grids; short explicit witnesses are stored.  Cycles pass as the 2D control.
- **Scope:** this is a finite family of natural layer graphs and the undeformed pair; it is not a theorem about all possible deformations.
- **Evidence:** `proofs/tridiagonal_nogo.md`, `results/tridiagonal_certificate.json`, `results/tridiagonal_certificate_general.json`.

### Universal one-row tridiagonal certificate — falsified and corrected

- **Route:** use one purportedly universal quartic Pauli row for every rectangular layer.
- **Counterevidence:** it works for all `4x4` strings and half the strings when one side is 4; `3x3`, `3x5`, `3x6`, and `5x5` need four-row certificates.
- **Learned:** the no-solution theorem can survive failure of a cosmetic certificate pattern; store the actual minimal witness per geometry.
- **Evidence:** `experiments/e22_tridiagonal_certificate.py`, `experiments/e23_certificate_general.py`, `proofs/tridiagonal_nogo.md`.

## Other integrability diagnostics

### Translation-invariant local Pauli charge tower — absent in tested range

- **Route:** find the 2D-layer analogue of the growing 1D free-fermion conserved-charge tower.
- **Outcome:** 1D controls return nontrivial dimensions `1,3,5,7`; the 2D square layer gives only `span{I,H}` through connected-cover range 4 in an ansatz of dimension 2461, at generic fields and two primes.
- **Scope:** bounded local Pauli ansatz only; this is not a theorem excluding nonlocal or non-Pauli charges.
- **Evidence:** `notes/integrability.md`, `results/integrability/conserved_charges.json`.

### Unrestricted full transfer commutant as an integrability test — diagnostically failed

- **Route:** use the dimension of the full matrix commutant or spectral degeneracy as evidence of integrability.
- **Problem:** generic matrices with simple spectra already have a large polynomial commutant; global spin flip and lattice symmetries add nonlocal blocks.
- **Learned:** conserved charges must be constrained by locality/structure.  The finite spectrum calculation is recorded but not interpreted as a no-go theorem.
- **Evidence:** `notes/integrability.md`, `results/integrability/conserved_charges.json`.

### Constant tetrahedron equation in the tested delta-bond tensor — fails

- **Route:** specialize a constant tetrahedron equation to the isotropic bond-dimensional-2 3D Ising tensor.
- **Outcome:** an exact residual polynomial is nonzero; allowed orthogonal/monomial gauges do not remove it.
- **Scope:** broad GL(2) transformations alter the copied-spin bond metric, and spectral-parameter or different tensor representations are not covered.
- **Evidence:** `notes/integrability.md`, `results/integrability/tetrahedron.json`.

### “Only two sectors in the full summed-pair commutant” — falsified and corrected

- **Route:** infer from the individual-term Pauli centraliser `{I, global X}` that the full commutant of the sums `A,B` has only two sectors.
- **Counterexample:** one-site translation on `C3` commutes exactly with both sums and is linearly independent of the two Pauli symmetries (Gram determinant `480`).
- **Correct scope:** the theorem excludes Kitaev-like **Pauli fluxes commuting termwise**; it does not exclude momentum sectors, other non-Pauli operators, or ancilla constructions.
- **Evidence:** `proofs/algebraic_obstruction.md`, `results/audit/audit_replication.json`, `results/flux_sectors.json`.

  rejects its first useful challenge point. Both are below the current wave-11 SAW endpoint

### The last HT Euler-ODE candidate relation — refuted at its first unobservable coefficient

- **Route:** the single wave-9 high-temperature Euler-ODE row (order 2, degree 6, 21 columns) whose
