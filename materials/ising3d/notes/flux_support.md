# Support-resolved conserved operators for finite Ising layers

## Scope and outcome

For a finite graph `G=(V,E)`, define

\[
A=\sum_{v\in V}X_v,
\qquad
B=\sum_{\{u,v\}\in E}Z_uZ_v.
\]

An operator has **site support contained in** `S` when it is of the form
`O=O_S tensor I_(V\S)`.  We classify the joint kernel of `ad_A` and `ad_B`
on every connected `S` in the stated finite layers, quotienting support sets
only by automorphisms of the full graph.  The machine-readable table is
`results/integrability/flux_support.json`; it is generated by
`experiments/e57_flux_support.py` and sampled independently by
`tests/test_flux_support.py`.

**[THEOREM — support-resolved scalar vacuum].** Exact certificates prove:

| open layer | sites | every connected `S` with `2 <= |S| <=` | conclusion |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `2x4` | 8 | **7** | the joint kernel on `End(H_S)` is `span_Q{I}` |
| `3x3` | 9 | **6** | the joint kernel on `End(H_S)` is `span_Q{I}` |
| `3x4` | 12 | **7** | the joint kernel on `End(H_S)` is `span_Q{I}` |

Because the ordered Pauli basis is a rational basis of the full complex
matrix algebra, scalar kernel over `Q` extends to scalar kernel over `C`.
Thus these are not Pauli-string-only statements: arbitrary linear
combinations of all `4^|S|` Pauli monomials were allowed.

## Exact local linear system

**[LEMMA — localization including boundary bonds].** Put
`A_S=sum_(v in S) X_v`, and split the bonds into those outside `S` and those
incident to `S`.  Since `O` is the identity outside `S`,

\[
[O,A]=[O,A_S],
\]

and every bond entirely outside `S` has zero commutator with `O`.  Therefore

\[
[O,B]
 =\sum_{\{u,v\}\in E:\{u,v\}\cap S\ne\varnothing}
   [O,Z_uZ_v].
\]

This includes all internal bonds and all boundary-crossing bonds.  On a
boundary bond, the outside `Z` remains in the output Pauli word; the code
retains global output masks so it cannot be erased by projecting to `S`.
Thus `[O,A]=[O,B]=0` is exactly the stacked local system implemented by the
experiment.

Write an arbitrary operator on `S` in the ordered monomial basis

\[
O=\sum_{a,b\subseteq S}c_{a,b}Q_{a,b},
\qquad Q_{a,b}=X^aZ^b,
\]

with `4^|S|` rational unknowns.  Extending `a,b` by zero outside `S`, the
normalized equations are

\[
\frac12[A,Q_{a,b}]
 =\sum_{v\in S:b_v=1}Q_{a+e_v,b},
\]

and

\[
\frac12[B,Q_{a,b}]
 =-\sum_{\{u,v\}\in E:\{u,v\}\cap S\ne\varnothing\atop a_u+a_v=1}
 Q_{a,b+e_u+e_v}.
\]

Every coefficient is `0`, `+1`, or `-1`; no floating-point arithmetic enters.
For scalar rows, either prime gives nullity one.  Since an integer matrix has
`rank_Fp <= rank_Q`, its rational nullity is at most the modular nullity; the
explicit identity gives rational nullity at least one.  Hence every reported
scalar row is exactly `span_Q{I}`.  The second prime independently reproduces
every nullity.

## Seven-site `3x3` charges: the hoped-for extension is false

**[COMPUTATION — exact local-charge discovery, parent-review flag].** At
seven sites the `3x3` scalar vacuum ends.  Four symmetry-inequivalent supports
have non-scalar kernels.  Sites are row-major `0,1,2 / 3,4,5 / 6,7,8`.

| support mask | sites | omitted sites | exact kernel dimension |
|---:|---|---|---:|
| `239` | `0,1,2,3,5,6,7` | `4,8` | **4** |
| `254` | `1,2,3,4,5,6,7` | `0,8` | **4** |
| `367` | `0,1,2,3,5,6,8` | `4,7` | **2** |
| `381` | `0,2,3,4,5,6,8` | `1,7` | **2** |

This is not a two-prime inference.  For each exceptional support, sparse
Gaussian elimination was redone over `Fraction`; the complete basis is stored
term by term as `(a_mask,b_mask,numerator,denominator)`.  All coefficients are
`+1` or `-1`.  The four basis-support-size lists are respectively
`[1,28,35,64]`, `[1,28,35,64]`, `[1,63]`, and `[1,63]`.  Every stored vector
was substituted into every integer equation with zero residual, and its exact
rank equals the displayed kernel dimension.  Both prime nullities agree with
the exact rational dimensions.

These operators are not polynomials in the predecessor's supported Pauli
symmetries: Theorem 11 of `proofs/algebraic_obstruction.md` permits only `I`
and the full-graph `prod_v X_v`, while the latter has support nine and is not
an operator on any proper seven-site `S`.  In particular, each stored
nonidentity basis vector contains 28, 35, 63, or 64 Pauli monomials and is not
an individual Pauli flux.  Some of the excess is compatible with spatial
intertwiners, but the complete exact bases—not an interpretation by a guessed
symmetry—are the certificate.

**[UNRESOLVED].** Whether these finite `3x3` seven-site charges extend to a
uniform larger-layer local density or are only small-layer spatial/module
symmetries is unresolved.  Their absence on every seven-site support of
`3x4` is exact evidence against direct extension, but is not an all-size
proof.

## Controls

**[COMPUTATION — 1D free-fermion control].** The same exact system on the
full four-site open chain has kernel dimension **5**, agreeing with the known
finite-chain joint commutant and detecting nontrivial conserved structure
rather than forcing scalar answers by construction.  Its complete rational
basis is stored and exactly substituted.

A proper connected interval of a longer open chain is scalar because its
boundary bonds pin the interval; the known free-fermion hierarchy consists
of extensive sums or finite-chain full-support operators.  Accordingly the
full `P4` control, rather than a boundary-cut interval, is the relevant test
of the machinery.

**[COMPUTATION — `C4` symmetry control].** For `C4` (the `2x2` layer), a
three-site connected support already has exact kernel dimension **3**, and
the full-support kernel has dimension **27**.  Both exact bases commute term
by term.  The control therefore detects the expected extra cycle symmetry.

## Relation to the Pauli-centraliser theorem and transfer family

The predecessor, Theorem 11 and Lemma 12 in
`proofs/algebraic_obstruction.md`, classifies individual Pauli strings:
for a connected graph, only `I` and `prod_v X_v` commute with the term set,
and a Pauli string commutes with each sum iff it commutes term by term.  The
present theorem removes the individual-Pauli restriction at bounded proper
support by solving for arbitrary operator coefficients and allowing all
cancellations among Pauli monomials.

A coupling-independent operator commutes with the analytic one-parameter
transfer family generated by `A` and `B` iff it commutes with both generators
(equivalently, differentiate the family at the identity to recover the two
infinitesimal generators).  The tables therefore certify a support-resolved
vacuum for coupling-independent conserved charges through the stated
budgets.  This is a physical obstruction to a conventional local-charge
tower, not a theorem that the 3D Ising model is non-integrable or unsolvable.

## Honest computational wall

**[UNRESOLVED — size eight and beyond].** The scan stops at `|S|=7`.  A
size-eight support has `4^8=65536` unknowns, and `3x4` has **66**
symmetry-inequivalent connected size-eight supports.  In a measured trial,
the generic sparse-column method that is fast through seven suffered severe
fill-in on the full `2x4` size-eight map and required about 156 seconds for
one modular rank.  Exhausting 66 representatives at two primes plus exact
verification of any excess was not credible on the contended machine.

No claim is made for disconnected supports, support size eight or larger,
quasilocal/nonlocal charges, coupling-dependent operators not jointly
commuting with `A,B`, or thermodynamic integrability.  The finite theorem and
the four exact negative-to-the-expected-outcome certificates are the complete
scope.


sampled stored witnesses exactly.
