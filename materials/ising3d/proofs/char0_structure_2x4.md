# Exact characteristic-zero structure of the open 2x4 layer

## Theorem and scope

This note concerns only the finite open `2x4` graph, with

\[
A=\sum_{v=1}^{8}X_v,\qquad B=\sum_{(u,v)\in E}Z_uZ_v.
\]

It makes no assertion for other widths or layer sizes.

**[THEOREM]** Over \(\mathbb Q\), the finite open-`2x4` Ising layer Lie algebra has

\[
\dim\mathfrak g=2952,
\qquad
\mathfrak g=Z(\mathfrak g)\oplus[\mathfrak g,\mathfrak g],
\qquad
\dim Z(\mathfrak g)=1,
\]

and

\[
[\mathfrak g,\mathfrak g]
\cong
D_{21}\oplus B_{13}\oplus B_{13}\oplus A_{23}
\oplus A_{27}\oplus A_3\oplus A_3.
\]

Thus the Levi dimension is

\[
861+351+351+575+783+15+15=2951,
\]

the Levi rank is

\[
21+13+13+23+27+3+3=103,
\]

the solvable radical is exactly the one-dimensional centre, and the Killing form has exact rational rank `2951` and nullity `1`.

The machine-readable certificate is `results/algebra_structure/char0_levi_2x4.json`, generated by `experiments/e49_char0_levi_2x4.py`.

## Exact symmetry-sector representation

Let

\[
G=C_2(\text{row reflection})\times C_2(\text{column reflection})
\times C_2(\text{global spin flip}).
\]

For each character \(\chi\in\widehat G\), the script constructs an integer orbit basis of the \(\chi\)-isotypic computational-basis sector. The orbit vector of a representative \(s\) is

\[
v_{s,\chi}=\sum_{g\in G}(-1)^{\chi\cdot g}\lvert gs\rangle,
\]

normalized so that its representative coefficient is one. Direct integer action gives exact matrices \((A_\chi,B_\chi)\). Their dimensions, in character order `000,001,...,111`, are

\[
(44,32,28,32,28,32,28,32).
\]

Their direct sum is the full 256-dimensional physical representation and is faithful on \(\mathfrak g\).

## Orthogonal image factors

For a sector pair, the common module kernel is computed over \(\mathbb Q\) as

\[
\ker A_\chi\cap\ker B_\chi
 =\ker\begin{bmatrix}A_\chi\\B_\chi\end{bmatrix}.
\]

An exact rational change of basis puts this kernel first and gives the faithful quotient action. Symmetric invariant forms solve

\[
A_\chi^T S+SA_\chi=0,
\qquad B_\chi^T S+SB_\chi=0,
\qquad S^T=S.
\]

**[COMPUTATION]** On `000`, the common kernel has dimension 2. The quotient has dimension 42. Its invariant symmetric-form space is one-dimensional, and the stored primitive integer form has exact rank 42. Therefore the image lies in \(\mathfrak{so}_{42}(\mathbb Q)\), of dimension 861.

A stored nonzero modular minor has rank 861. Since modular rank is a lower bound for rational rank,

\[
861\le\dim_{\mathbb Q}\operatorname{im}_{000}
\le\dim\mathfrak{so}_{42}=861.
\]

**[THEOREM]** The `000` image equals \(\mathfrak{so}_{42}\), type \(D_{21}\).

**[COMPUTATION]** On each of `010`, `100`, and `110`, the common kernel has dimension 1. Each quotient has dimension 27, with a unique nondegenerate invariant symmetric form. Thus each image lies in \(\mathfrak{so}_{27}\), dimension 351. The stored modular rank is 351, so the same upper/lower argument gives equality.

**[THEOREM]** Each of these three sector images is \(\mathfrak{so}_{27}\), type \(B_{13}\).

The exact full-rank intertwiner between `100` and `110` proves that those two sectors carry the same \(B_{13}\) factor. The exact joint modular rank of `010+100` is 702, while the exact upper bound is \(351+351=702\); hence `010` carries an independent second \(B_{13}\) factor.

## The 606- and 799-dimensional images

The commutant of a sector is solved from

\[
XA_\chi=A_\chi X,
\qquad XB_\chi=B_\chi X
\]

over \(\mathbb Q\). A rational separating element of this commutant yields exact rational spectral projectors. The standalone test verifies every projector is idempotent and that the projectors sum to the identity.

**[COMPUTATION]** The exact commutant dimensions and projector ranks are

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{sector}&\dim\operatorname{Comm}&\text{projector ranks}\\\hline
001&3&4,4,24\\
111&3&4,4,24\\
011&2&4,28\\
101&2&4,28.
\end{array}
\]

On a multiplicity-free block decomposition of dimensions \(d_i\), every commutator is traceless separately on every block. The two generators have a one-dimensional trace image across the blocks. Therefore the exact image upper bounds are

\[
1+(4^2-1)+(4^2-1)+(24^2-1)=606
\]

for `001` and `111`, and

\[
1+(4^2-1)+(28^2-1)=799
\]

for `011` and `101`. The prior stored modular ranks are respectively 606 and 799, hence these upper bounds are equalities over \(\mathbb Q\).

**[THEOREM]** The derived image types are

\[
001,111:A_3\oplus A_3\oplus A_{23},
\qquad
011,101:A_3\oplus A_{27},
\]

with one common scalar image direction added in each full sector image.

## Exact linkage of repeated constituents

Abstract dimensions cannot be added until repeated sector constituents are linked. The script solves exact intertwiner systems between every needed constituent. All positive linkages below are represented by stored primitive integer full-rank matrices and are independently recomputed in the standalone test.

**[COMPUTATION]** `001` and `111` pair componentwise by contragredient full-rank intertwiners on dimensions `4,4,24`. Hence they show the same two \(A_3\) factors and the same \(A_{23}\) factor.

**[COMPUTATION]** `011` and `101` pair contragrediently on dimensions `4,28`. Hence they show the same \(A_3\) and \(A_{27}\) factors.

**[COMPUTATION]** The first 4-dimensional `001` constituent is directly equivalent to the 4-dimensional `011` constituent, so one \(A_3\) factor is shared across the 606- and 799-dimensional images. Exact direct and dual intertwiner spaces from the second `001` 4-space to the `011` 4-space are both zero, so the second \(A_3\) remains independent.

The resulting odd-sector semisimple dimension is

\[
575+783+15+15=1388,
\]

and the one scalar direction makes the full odd-sector image dimension 1389. This equals the stored modular joint rank of `001+011`; the exact rational upper and modular lower bounds therefore meet.

## Complete dimension and Levi type

The exact linked image upper bound across all eight sectors is

\[
861+702+1389=2952.
\]

The stored modular closure rank 2952 is a rigorous rational lower bound. Therefore

\[
\dim_{\mathbb Q}\mathfrak g=2952.
\]

Every commutator is orthogonal or blockwise traceless, so the exact linked upper bound for the derived algebra is

\[
861+702+1388=2951.
\]

The stored modular derived rank 2951 is a rigorous lower bound. Therefore

\[
\dim_{\mathbb Q}[\mathfrak g,\mathfrak g]=2951,
\]

and equality in every classical/block upper bound identifies

\[
[\mathfrak g,\mathfrak g]
\cong D_{21}\oplus B_{13}^{\oplus2}\oplus A_{23}
\oplus A_{27}\oplus A_3^{\oplus2}.
\]

Each displayed classical factor is simple in characteristic zero, so the derived algebra is semisimple.

## Centre, radical, and Killing form

The block-scalar trace part of \(B\) is reconstructed exactly from the rational sector projectors. It is zero on the orthogonal sectors and nonzero on the four odd sectors. Every stored block-scalar matrix commutes exactly with both sector generators. Subtracting this block-scalar element from \(B\) leaves its componentwise traceless part, which lies in the proved derived algebra. Thus this is a nonzero central complement to the codimension-one derived algebra.

**[THEOREM]** \(Z(\mathfrak g)\) has dimension 1 and

\[
\mathfrak g=Z(\mathfrak g)\oplus[\mathfrak g,\mathfrak g].
\]

Consequently every solvable ideal intersects the semisimple derived algebra trivially, while the one-dimensional central summand is solvable.

**[THEOREM]**

\[
\operatorname{rad}(\mathfrak g)=Z(\mathfrak g),
\qquad \dim\operatorname{rad}(\mathfrak g)=1.
\]

The Killing form vanishes on the centre and is nondegenerate on the semisimple derived algebra. Hence:

**[THEOREM]** The exact rational Killing rank is 2951 and its nullity is 1.

This is a structural exact certificate, not a materialized 2951-by-2951 minor. The requested direct minor/null-vector construction was attempted but hit the measured resource wall described below. The artifact labels that missing direct certificate `[UNRESOLVED]`; it is not needed for the structural rank theorem.

## Resource wall for the direct Killing route

**[COMPUTATION]** A modular word-basis probe selects 2952 independent nested commutator words at prime `2147483647`, with maximum depth 21. Repeated runs took 83.49--120.54 seconds.

**[COMPUTATION]** Converting the two generator actions to this word basis took 115.38 seconds and produced `1,160,908` and `1,000,657` nonzero entries. A deterministic sparse `DomainMatrix` common-kernel attempt was stopped after more than 3600 seconds at approximately 660,242,432 bytes RSS; it produced no certificate and no partial output from it is used.

A dense collection of all 2952 adjoint matrices would occupy

\[
2952^3\cdot8=205,797,003,264\text{ bytes}\approx191.66\text{ GiB}
\]

as `int64`, before elimination overhead. The approximately 70 MB final Killing array is itself feasible, but the direct adjoint/trace and minor construction was not completed. Exact sector factorization avoids that wall.
