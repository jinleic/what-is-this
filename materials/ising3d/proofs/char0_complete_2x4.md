# Complete characteristic-zero structure of the open 2x4 layer

## Scope and statement

Let

\[
 A=\sum_{v=1}^{8}X_v,\qquad
 B=\sum_{(u,v)\in E(2\times4)}Z_uZ_v
\]

on the finite **open** `2x4` layer, and let \(\mathfrak g=\operatorname{Lie}_{\mathbb Q}\langle A,B\rangle\). This note concerns this one finite graph only; it makes no all-width or thermodynamic assertion.

**[THEOREM]** Let \(F_{000},F_{010},F_{100}\) be the exact nondegenerate
rational symmetric forms stored by the certificate (the `100` and `110`
modules are exactly intertwined and use the same orthogonal factor). There is
an isomorphism of rational Lie algebras

\[
 \mathfrak g\cong \mathbb Qz\oplus
 \mathfrak{so}(F_{000},\mathbb Q)\oplus
 \mathfrak{so}(F_{010},\mathbb Q)\oplus
 \mathfrak{so}(F_{100},\mathbb Q)\oplus
 \mathfrak{sl}_{24}(\mathbb Q)\oplus
 \mathfrak{sl}_{28}(\mathbb Q)\oplus
 \mathfrak{sl}_{4}(\mathbb Q)^{\oplus2}.
\]

The absolute Dynkin types are
\[
 D_{21}+2B_{13}+A_{23}+A_{27}+2A_3.
\]
No claim is made that the three orthogonal rational forms are split over
\(\mathbb Q\).

The dimension and rank identities are

\[
 2952=1+861+2(351)+575+783+2(15),
 \qquad
 \operatorname{rank}(\mathfrak g')=21+2(13)+23+27+2(3)=103.
\]

**[THEOREM]** The centre and solvable radical are the same one-dimensional subspace, \(Z(\mathfrak g)=\operatorname{rad}(\mathfrak g)=\mathbb Qz\).

**[THEOREM]** The characteristic-zero Killing form has rank \(2951\) and nullity one.

Echelon rows decide whether a raw word is accepted, but accepted words are retained by their parent/action recipes. Thus this is a rank calculation on actual integer/rational Lie words, not on unrelated modular vectors.

**[LEMMA]** If integer vectors have rank \(r\) after reduction modulo a prime, the corresponding rational vectors have rank at least \(r\): a nonzero modular pivot minor is the reduction of a nonzero integer minor. Therefore

\[
 \dim_{\mathbb Q}\mathfrak g\ge2952,
 \qquad
 \dim_{\mathbb Q}\mathfrak g'\ge2951.
\]

## 2. The faithful exact-Q sector representation

Row reflection, column reflection, and global spin flip generate \(C_2^3\). Exact character projection decomposes the complete 256-dimensional physical representation into sector module dimensions

\[
(44,32,28,32,28,32,28,32)
\]

in character order `000,001,010,011,100,101,110,111`.

**[LEMMA]** Their direct sum is a change of basis of the complete physical representation. The induced representation of \(\mathfrak g\), regarded as the concrete Lie algebra of physical matrices generated by \(A,B\), is therefore faithful.

### 2.1 Exact common-kernel splittings

Each sector basis has a positive diagonal rational Gram matrix \(G\), inherited from the standard physical inner product. The generator matrices obey

\[
 A^TG=GA,\qquad B^TG=GB.
\]

If \(K=\ker A\cap\ker B\), the exact \(G\)-orthogonal complement \(K^{\perp_G}\) is invariant under both generators. The experiment constructs exact integer bases for \(K\) and \(K^{\perp_G}\), proves their concatenation has full rank, changes basis over \(\mathbb Q\), and verifies that both off-diagonal action blocks vanish.

**[COMPUTATION]** The common-kernel dimensions are two in sector `000`, one in each of `010,100,110`, and zero in the four odd sectors. Hence the active natural-module dimensions are 42 and 27,27,27 respectively. This exact splitting is important: there is no unaccounted extension cocycle from a zero submodule to the active quotient.

### 2.2 Orthogonal factors

On the 42-dimensional `000` active summand, exact rational nullspace computation for

\[
 X^TS+SX=0\quad(X=A,B)
\]

produces a unique (up to scale) nondegenerate symmetric form \(F_{000}\).
Thus the image is contained in \(\mathfrak{so}(F_{000},\mathbb Q)\), of
dimension 861 and absolute type \(D_{21}\). The literal-word modular minor has
rank 861, so the containment is equality over \(\mathbb Q\).

The same computation on the active 27-dimensional `010`, `100`, and `110`
summands produces unique nondegenerate symmetric forms. Each individual image
is exactly the corresponding orthogonal rational form
\(\mathfrak{so}(F,\mathbb Q)\), dimension 351 and absolute type \(B_{13}\).
Exact intertwiners and raw-word joint minors prove that `100` and `110` are the
same factor, while `010+100` has joint dimension \(702=351+351\). Thus these
three sectors contribute exactly two independent \(B_{13}\) factors. Their
splitness over \(\mathbb Q\) is not asserted.

**[THEOREM]** The even-sector semisimple image is

\[
 D_{21}+B_{13}+B_{13},\qquad
 \text{dimension }861+351+351=1563.
\]

### 2.3 Odd-sector type and linkage

Exact rational commutants are separated by rational spectral projectors. The constituent dimensions are

\[
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
\text{sector}&001&111&011&101\\\hline
\text{constituents}&4+4+24&4+4+24&4+28&4+28.

\]

The projectors are stored exactly and rechecked for idempotence, orthogonality, completeness, and generator invariance. On each constituent, the traceless image is contained in \(\mathfrak{sl}_d\). The generator block-trace matrix has exact rank one in every full odd sector, so its full image is contained in

\[
 \mathbb Qc+\bigoplus_i\mathfrak{sl}_{d_i}.
\]

The corresponding raw-word minors meet those upper dimensions: 606 for `001/111` and 799 for `011/101`. Hence each displayed block container is exact over \(\mathbb Q\); this is not a classification inferred from dimension alone.

Exact intertwiner equations are solved over \(\mathbb Q\), and explicit full-rank integer witnesses are stored:

* the `001` and `111` constituents are paired componentwise in the dual convention, with witness ranks 4,4,24;
* `011` and `101` are paired componentwise in the dual convention, with ranks 4,28;
* one 4-dimensional constituent of `001` is directly identified with the 4-dimensional constituent of `011`;
* the remaining 4-dimensional constituent is independent; the raw-word joint `001+011` minor has rank 1389 and closes the complete linkage count.

It follows that the odd sectors contribute one common central trace line and

\[
 A_{23}+A_{27}+A_3+A_3,
 \qquad\text{dimension}\qquad
 1+575+783+15+15=1389.
\]

**[THEOREM]** Combining even and odd contributions yields a faithful containing algebra of dimension

\[
1563+1389=2952.
\]

## 3. Equality, factor-map kernel, and the radical

The faithful exact-Q sector representation puts \(\mathfrak g\) inside the 2952-dimensional linked container. Section 1 gives \(\dim_{\mathbb Q}\mathfrak g\ge2952\), so equality holds:

\[
\dim_{\mathbb Q}\mathfrak g=2952.
\]

The traceless semisimple part of the container has dimension 2951. The derived-word certificate gives \(\dim_{\mathbb Q}\mathfrak g'\ge2951\), and containment gives the reverse inequality. Consequently

\[
\mathfrak g'=D_{21}+2B_{13}+A_{23}+A_{27}+2A_3
\]

exactly over \(\mathbb Q\).

The experiment constructs the exact scalar projection \(z\) of physical \(B\)
on every rational constituent and stores one globally normalized integer
matrix together with the single rational scale relating it to \(z\). Exact
multiplication gives \([z,A]=[z,B]=0\). Moreover \(B-z\) is traceless on every
simple constituent, hence lies in the 2951-dimensional semisimple container.
The independently certified derived image has the same dimension 2951, so it
equals that container and \(B-z\in\mathfrak g'\). Therefore
\(z=B-(B-z)\in\mathfrak g\); this is an exact membership proof, not an
assumption based on commuting matrices.

**[THEOREM]** Projection of the faithful image onto the seven simple rational
factors has exact joint rank 2951 and kernel precisely \(\mathbb Qz\).

**[LEMMA]** If a Lie algebra maps onto a semisimple algebra with central kernel \(C\), every solvable ideal maps to a solvable ideal of the semisimple quotient and hence maps to zero. Thus every solvable ideal lies in \(C\). Conversely \(C\) is solvable. Applying this to the exact factor map gives

\[
\operatorname{rad}(\mathfrak g)=Z(\mathfrak g)=C=\mathbb Qz.
\]

This is a quotient argument, not an unsupported invocation of Cartan's criterion.

## 4. Killing form

On a reductive direct sum \(C\oplus\bigoplus_i\mathfrak s_i\), the adjoint Killing form vanishes on the central summand and is nondegenerate on each characteristic-zero simple summand. The certified decomposition therefore reconstructs the Killing form structurally as a zero one-dimensional block plus seven nondegenerate simple blocks.

**[THEOREM]** The exact rational Killing rank is

\[
861+2(351)+575+783+2(15)=2951,
\]

and its kernel is the one-dimensional centre.

**[UNRESOLVED]** No direct `2952 x 2952` adjoint-trace Killing matrix or exact `2951 x 2951` minor was materialized. The theorem does not need that route: it follows from the independently certified characteristic-zero direct sum. The artifact marks the distinction explicitly and does not present reconstructed structural rank as a direct adjoint-array computation.
