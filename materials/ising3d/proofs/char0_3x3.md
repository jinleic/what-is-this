# Complete characteristic-zero structure of the open 3x3 layer

## Scope and statement

Let \(\Lambda=\{0,1,2\}^2\) be the finite **open** square grid, with its
12 nearest-neighbour edges, and put

\[
 A=\sum_{v\in\Lambda}X_v,\qquad
 B=\sum_{\{u,v\}\in E(\Lambda)}Z_uZ_v,
 \qquad \mathfrak g=\operatorname{Lie}_{\mathbb Q}\langle A,B\rangle.
\]

**[THEOREM]** For this one finite layer,

\[
\mathfrak g\cong \mathbb Qz\oplus
 \mathfrak{sl}_{33}(\mathbb Q)\oplus
 \mathfrak{sl}_{48}(\mathbb Q)\oplus
 \mathfrak{sl}_{59}(\mathbb Q)\oplus
 \mathfrak{sl}_{3}(\mathbb Q)\oplus
 \mathfrak{sl}_{16}(\mathbb Q)\oplus
 \mathfrak{sl}_{30}(\mathbb Q).
\]

Consequently,

\[
 \dim_{\mathbb Q}\mathfrak g=8034,\qquad
 \dim_{\mathbb Q}[\mathfrak g,\mathfrak g]=8033,
\]

and the derived algebra has absolute type

\[
 A_{32}+A_{47}+A_{58}+A_2+A_{15}+A_{29}.
\]

The centre and solvable radical are both \(\mathbb Qz\), the Levi rank is
\(32+47+58+2+15+29=183\), and the structural Killing-form rank/nullity are
\(8033/1\), respectively.  This is neither an all-layer-size result nor a
thermodynamic statement.

## Exact symmetry and sector construction

**[COMPUTATION]** The grid automorphism group generated by row reflection and
transpose is the order-eight group \(D_4\); it fixes \(A\) and \(B\).  Together
with global spin flip it contains the commuting subgroup

\[
 C_2(\text{row reflection})\times C_2(\text{column reflection})
 \times C_2(\text{global spin flip}).
\]

The exact character-sector dimensions of the 512-dimensional physical
representation, in character order \(000,001,010,011,100,101,110,111\), are

\[
 84,84,60,60,60,60,52,52.
\]

The certificate constructs these rational character bases and their Gram
matrices from orbit sums, then performs all projector, commutant-centre,
intertwiner, and form calculations over \(\mathbb Q\).  The transpose action
links \(010\leftrightarrow100\) and \(011\leftrightarrow101\).  Exact
spin-flip-sign and contragredient witnesses link the remaining paired
P-even/P-odd components.  Thus the eight matrix images are constrained by one
linked global container, rather than treated as eight unrelated algebras.

## Upper and lower certificates

### Exact rational upper container

**[THEOREM]** Exact rational commutant-centre projectors split the natural
modules into nontrivial dimensions

\[
 33,48,59,3,16,30.
\]

The exact linkage witnesses and the common scalar projection of \(A\) give a
faithful container

\[
 \mathbb Qz\oplus\bigoplus_{d\in\{33,48,59,3,16,30\}}
 \mathfrak{sl}_d(\mathbb Q),
\]

whose dimension is certified directly as

\[
 1+(33^2-1)+(48^2-1)+(59^2-1)+(3^2-1)+(16^2-1)+(30^2-1)=8034.
\]

The stored global integral central witness \(W\) is a 512-by-512 sparse matrix
with 1,840 nonzero entries and weighted checksum \(844388687\pmod{1{,}000{,}000{,}007}\).
It satisfies \(z=W/1869120\); exact commutators with both generators vanish.
The blockwise scalar projection gives \(A-z\) and \(B\) in the linked
block-traceless part.

The factor classification does not infer type from dimensions alone.  For each
representative natural module, exact commutant control gives a scalar
traceless commutant, and both the symmetric and alternating invariant-form
nullities are zero.  Together with exact block-traceless containment and the
faithful dimension squeeze, this identifies the rational factors as the
listed split \(\mathfrak{sl}_d(\mathbb Q)\)'s.

### Literal Pauli-word lower minors

**[COMPUTATION]** At the good prime
\(p=2{,}147{,}483{,}647\), the producer constructs the same nested Pauli-word
tree using only \([A,w]/2\) and \([B,w]/2\).  All accepted rows are literal
integer Pauli words reduced modulo \(p\), so a nonzero pivot minor is a
rigorous lower bound over \(\mathbb Q\).

| closure | \(\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_p}\) | four grading-block ranks | max depth | tree checksum mod \(1{,}000{,}000{,}007\) |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|
| \(\langle A,B\rangle\) | 8034 | \((1962,1963,2147,1962)\) | 21 | 485501005 |
| derived seed \([B,A]/2\) | 8033 | \((1962,1963,2146,1962)\) | 21 | 712911640 |

The orbit support has 8,739 exact \(D_4\)-orbits.  Hence the two raw-word
minors prove \(\dim_{\mathbb Q}\mathfrak g\ge8034\) and
\(\dim_{\mathbb Q}\mathfrak g'\ge8033\).  The rational container proves the
opposite upper bounds, so equality follows.  In particular \(\mathfrak g'\)
equals the block-traceless linked container and \(z\in\mathfrak g\).

## Centre, radical, and Killing form

**[THEOREM]** The faithful factor projection has kernel exactly \(\mathbb Qz\)
and quotient equal to the displayed split semisimple direct sum.  Every
solvable ideal maps trivially to that quotient, so

\[
 \operatorname{rad}(\mathfrak g)=Z(\mathfrak g)=\mathbb Qz.
\]

The Killing conclusion is reconstructed from this exact direct-sum result:
it vanishes on the central line and is nondegenerate on each simple
\(\mathfrak{sl}_d\) factor.  Thus its rank is 8033 and nullity is 1.

**[UNRESOLVED]** No dense \(8034\times8034\) adjoint-trace matrix or direct
adjoint minor was materialized; the Killing statement is structural, not a
claim that such an array was computed.  A direct dense literal matrix-word
minor for the 84-dimensional sector at \(p=65521\) was stopped at a
3,600-second wall.  It is not needed for the theorem, because the exact
all-sector faithful squeeze supplies the common-basis upper bound and the raw
Pauli minor supplies the lower bound.
