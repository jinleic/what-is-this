# Exact characteristic-zero structure of the 2×3 open-layer algebra

## Scope and statement

Let
\[
A=\sum_{v=0}^{5}X_v,\qquad B=\sum_{\{i,j\}\in E}Z_iZ_j,
\]
where
\(E=\{03,14,25,01,12,34,45\}\) is the seven-edge 2×3 open grid, and let
\(\mathfrak g=\langle A,B\rangle_{\mathrm{Lie}}\) over \(\mathbb Q\).
Everything below concerns this **one finite six-spin representation**. It is not
an all-layer-size statement.

**[COMPUTATION — exact finite object]**
\[
\dim_{\mathbb Q}\mathfrak g=263,
\quad \dim[\mathfrak g,\mathfrak g]=262,
\quad \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Q}\kappa=262,
\quad \dim\operatorname{rad}\mathfrak g=1.
\]
The radical is the centre, and
\[
\mathfrak g/\operatorname{rad}\mathfrak g
 \cong \mathfrak{sp}_{14}\oplus\mathfrak{sp}_{6}\oplus
 \mathfrak{sp}_{6}\oplus\mathfrak{sl}_{9}\oplus\mathfrak{sl}_{6}
\]
over \(\mathbb Q\), of type \(C_7+C_3+C_3+A_8+A_5\). The factor dimensions
are \(105,21,21,80,35\), summing to 262; the total rank is 26.

The exact certificate is produced by `experiments/e45_char0_levi.py`, recorded
in `results/algebra_structure/char0_levi.json`, and independently re-derived by
`tests/test_char0_levi.py`.

## 1. Exact Pauli closure and its orbit normalization

Use the repository's ordered-monomial Pauli strings
\[
Q_{a,b}=X^aZ^b,\qquad (a,b)\in\mathbb F_2^{12}.
\]
Their exact product law is
\(Q_{a,b}Q_{a',b'}=(-1)^{b\cdot a'}Q_{a+a',b+b'}\).
Thus a bracket is zero when the binary symplectic product vanishes and
otherwise is \(\pm2Q_{a+a',b+b'}\). Every Lie word therefore has integer
Pauli-coordinate entries. The span computation is exact over \(\mathbb Q\);
no floating point and no modular inference enters the dimension claim.

Let \(G=C_2\times C_2\) be generated by the horizontal and vertical grid
reflections. Both \(A\) and \(B\) are fixed by \(G\). The 1056 reachable Pauli
strings split into 304 \(G\)-orbits (8 of size 1, 68 of size 2, 228 of size 4).
For an orbit \(O\), put \(E_O=\sum_{q\in O}Q_q\).

**[LEMMA — orbit-coordinate equivariance]** For \(C\in\{A,B\}\), the
coefficient of \(Q_t\) in \([C,E_O]\) is constant as \(t\) ranges over a target
orbit \(O'\). Consequently
\([C,E_O]=\sum_{O'}c_{O',O}E_{O'}\), where
\[
c_{O',O}={1\over |O'|}\sum_{t\in O'} [Q_t]\,[C,E_O].
\]

*Proof.* For \(h\in G\), conjugation/permutation gives
\(hCh^{-1}=C\), \(hE_Oh^{-1}=E_O\), and hence
\(h[C,E_O]h^{-1}=[C,E_O]\). Conjugation sends \(Q_t\) to \(Q_{h(t)}\)
without an additional phase because it only permutes tensor factors. Therefore
the two coefficients are equal. Summing the equal coefficients over \(O'\)
gives the displayed normalization. ∎

The implementation does not rely on a representative shortcut: it explicitly
loops over **every source string in every orbit**, accumulates every bracket,
and asserts equality of all coefficients in each target orbit before assigning
the orbit coordinate. The standalone test assembles these full-orbit action
matrices a second time in independent code and closes their exact Fraction
span.

**[COMPUTATION]** This exact orbit-sum closure stops at dimension 263. Closure
under both \(\operatorname{ad}A\) and \(\operatorname{ad}B\) is checked after
every newly independent row. Independently, the eight exact symmetry-sector
matrix blocks yield 263 generated word matrices, whose 263×4096 rational
coordinate matrix has exact rank 263. These are separate computed checks in the
JSON (`Pauli_orbit_closure_dimension_Q` and `faithful_matrix_dimension_Q`).
Their agreement supplies both a direct Pauli certificate and a faithful-matrix
certificate.

## 2. Derived algebra, Killing form, and radical

The eight characters of the commuting row reflection, column reflection, and
global spin flip have exact sector dimensions
\[
14,10,6,6,6,10,6,6.
\]
The ideal generated by \([A,B]\), closed under \(\operatorname{ad}A\) and
\(\operatorname{ad}B\), has exact rational rank 262. This equals
\([\mathfrak g,\mathfrak g]\) because the latter is the smallest ideal
containing all brackets of the generators.

The factor representations below permit the Killing form to be built without
forming 263 dense adjoint matrices. For the natural representations,
\[
\kappa_{\mathfrak{sp}_{2n}}(x,y)=(2n+2)\operatorname{tr}(xy),\qquad
\kappa_{\mathfrak{sl}_{n}}(x,y)=2n\operatorname{tr}(xy).
\]
After exact projection away from the one-dimensional scalar constituents, the
script constructs
\[
\kappa=16T_{C_7}+8T_{C_{3,a}}+8T_{C_{3,b}}+18T_{A_8}+12T_{A_5}.
\]
In the recorded Lie-word basis this is a symmetric 263×263 **integer** matrix.
Exact Gauss–Jordan elimination over SymPy's `QQ` gives rank 262 and an exact
one-dimensional nullspace. The JSON records reproducible checksums of the full
matrix (not a replacement for recomputation): sum of absolute entries
779573214105401927016216, maximum absolute entry 89191495283996574613504, and
weighted residue 135248993 modulo 1000000007.

**[LEMMA — Cartan radical criterion]** For any finite-dimensional Lie algebra
\(L\) over a characteristic-zero field,
\[
\operatorname{rad}L=\{x\in L:\kappa_L(x,[L,L])=0\}.
\]

*Proof.* Extend scalars to an algebraic closure (both sides commute with this
extension). If \(R=\operatorname{rad}L\), Lie's theorem triangularizes
\(\operatorname{ad}R\) along a composition series, while
\(\operatorname{ad}[L,L]\) has zero diagonal on the induced solvable quotient;
therefore \(\operatorname{tr}(\operatorname{ad}x\operatorname{ad}y)=0\) for
\(x\in R,y\in[L,L]\). Conversely, pass to \(L/R\), which is semisimple by the
Levi theorem. Its Killing form is nondegenerate by Cartan's semisimplicity
criterion, and \([L/R,L/R]=L/R\); hence an element orthogonal to \([L,L]\)
maps to zero and lies in \(R\). ∎

**[COMPUTATION]** Section 3 certifies that the 262-dimensional derived algebra
is a direct sum of simple ideals, hence semisimple and perfect. Therefore the
criterion reduces here to the nullspace of the displayed Killing matrix. It is
one-dimensional. The same exact computation finds a one-dimensional common
kernel of \(\operatorname{ad}A\) and \(\operatorname{ad}B\), so the radical is
precisely the centre.

## 3. Exact Levi splitting and simplicity certificates

Choose five sector projections `000`, `010`, `100`, `001`, `011`. The joint
projection of the 262 derived basis vectors has exact rational rank 262, so it
is faithful. The individual exact images are as follows.

1. **`000`: \(C_7\), dimension 105.** The invariant alternating-form equations
   \(A^TF+FA=B^TF+FB=0\) have a one-dimensional exact nullspace. A primitive
   integral solution has \(\det F=16384\ne0\). Thus the image lies in
   \(\mathfrak{sp}(F)\), whose dimension is 105. Its computed image dimension
   is also 105, so the image equals \(\mathfrak{sp}_{14}\), which is simple.
2. **`010` and `100`: two independent \(C_3\) ideals, dimension 21 each.** Each
   has a unique invariant alternating form with determinant 16 and image
   dimension \(21=\dim\mathfrak{sp}_6\). The joint faithful rank shows these are
   distinct ideals; sector `110` repeats the `100` image.
3. **`001`: \(A_8\), dimension 80.** The exact commutant has dimension two and
   contains a rank-one idempotent. Its complementary rank-nine projector is
   computed rationally. The derived image kills the line, is traceless on the
   nine-space, and has exact dimension \(80=9^2-1\), hence it is all
   \(\mathfrak{sl}_9\), simple. Its natural trace form has exact rank 80.
4. **`011`: \(A_5\), dimension 35.** Its derived image is traceless on the
   six-space and has exact dimension \(35=6^2-1\), hence equals
   \(\mathfrak{sl}_6\), simple. Sector `111` repeats this image.

The five dimensions sum to the exact 262-dimensional joint rank. Thus there is
no unaccounted kernel, overlap, or further factor.

### Exact B7-versus-C7 distinction

Dimension 105 and rank 7 alone do **not** distinguish \(B_7\) from \(C_7\).
The decisive invariant here is representational: the `000` image acts
faithfully on a 14-dimensional rational space and preserves the certified
nondegenerate alternating form of determinant 16384. It therefore equals
\(\mathfrak{sp}_{14}\) by the exact dimension comparison. In characteristic
zero, \(B_7=\mathfrak{so}_{15}\) has no nontrivial 14-dimensional module (its
smallest nontrivial module is the 15-dimensional vector representation; the
spin module has dimension 128). Hence this factor is \(C_7\), not \(B_7\).

For an additional exact root invariant, take the standard Cartan element in the
seven symplectic pairs with eigenvalues
\(1,2,\ldots,7,-1,-2,\ldots,-7\). The script records the full 105-entry
\(\operatorname{ad}H\) eigenvalue multiset. It consists of a seven-dimensional
zero space and roots \(\pm(e_i-e_j),\pm(e_i+e_j),\pm2e_i\), the \(C_7\) root
system; the long roots \(\pm2e_i\) are visible exactly.

## 4. Meaning of the earlier modular witnesses

**[COMPUTATION]** The earlier finite-field `sp(6)` witnesses do lift: in
characteristic zero they are the two independent \(C_3\) ideals above (with a
third sector repeating one of them). The 36-dimensional `gl(6)` witness lifts
as the 35-dimensional \(A_5=\mathfrak{sl}_6\) factor together with the single
common central radical. It is not an extra 36-dimensional Levi ideal.

## 5. Verification, timings, and limitations

`tests/test_char0_levi.py` is standalone-runnable and does not read the result
JSON. It rebuilds all structure, and separately builds every full-orbit Pauli
action coefficient before checking dimension 263. It then re-derives the exact
Killing rank 262, radical dimension 1, all five factor dimensions, and the
\(C_7\) simplicity/form certificate. The latest experiment timings are stored
under `data.timings_seconds`; no 3000-second hard timeout was hit.

**[UNRESOLVED / explicitly out of scope]** Nothing here classifies the analogous
algebra for arbitrary rectangles or any thermodynamic-limit algebra. The result
is exact structure theory only for the specified finite 2×3 open layer.
