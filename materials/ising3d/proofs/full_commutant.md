# Full matrix commutants of finite Ising layers

## 1. Scope and outcome

For a finite graph `G=(V,E)`, put

\[
 A=\sum_{v\in V}X_v,\qquad B=\sum_{\{u,v\}\in E}Z_uZ_v,
\]

and define the **full matrix commutant**

\[
 \mathcal C(A,B)=\{T\in\operatorname{End}(\mathbb C^{2^{|V|}}):
 [T,A]=[T,B]=0\}.
\]

**[THEOREM — finite exact full-commutant dimensions].** For the specified open
finite graphs, exact rational certificates give

| graph | sites | `dim_C Comm(A,B)` |
|---|---:|---:|
| open chain | 4 | 5 |
| open chain | 5 | 6 |
| open chain | 6 | 7 |
| `2x2` open grid, equivalently the cycle `C4` | 4 | 27 |
| `2x3` open grid | 6 | **12** |
| `2x4` open grid | 8 | **41** |
| `3x3` open grid | 9 | **98** |

The theorem is only about these seven finite matrices. It does not assert a
formula for all rectangular sizes.

**[THEOREM — the proposed no-go is false].** On the `2x3` layer,

\[
 \mathcal C(A,B)\not=\operatorname{span}\{I,P\},\qquad
 P=\prod_{v=0}^{5}X_v.
\]

Indeed, its dimension is 12, not 2. Even before elimination, the two grid
reflections and `P` generate a represented `C2^3` whose group algebra has
dimension eight: all eight character sectors listed below are nonzero. The
exact computation finds four further dimensions.

**[THEOREM — algebra structure for `2x3`].** As a unital complex algebra,

\[
 \boxed{\mathcal C(A,B)\cong M_2(\mathbb C)\oplus\mathbb C^8},
 \qquad \dim Z(\mathcal C)=9.
\]

Thus the strongest finite conserved-charge no-go contemplated in the question
does **not** hold. There are exactly twelve coupling-independent full-matrix
charges, not merely functions of global parity.

The certificate is generated by
`experiments/e52_full_commutant.py`, stored in
`results/integrability/full_commutant.json`, and independently checked by
`tests/test_full_commutant.py`.

## 2. The exact `8192 x 4096` map for `2x3`

**[LEMMA — ordered-Pauli commutator formula].** Let

\[
 Q_{a,b}=X^aZ^b,\qquad (a,b)\in\mathbb F_2^n\times\mathbb F_2^n.
\]

Then

\[
 \frac12[X_i,Q_{a,b}]
 =\begin{cases}Q_{a+e_i,b},&b_i=1,\\0,&b_i=0,\end{cases}
\]

and

\[
 \frac12[Z_iZ_j,Q_{a,b}]
 =\begin{cases}-Q_{a,b+e_i+e_j},&a_i+a_j=1,\\0,&a_i+a_j=0.
 \end{cases}
\]

*Proof.* Move `X_i`, respectively `Z_iZ_j`, through the ordered monomial.
The sign is `(-1)^{b_i}`, respectively `(-1)^{a_i+a_j}`. In the
anticommuting case the two orderings differ by two; otherwise they cancel. ∎

**[LEMMA — rational and complex nullities agree].** The `4^n` ordered
monomials are a basis of `Mat_(2^n)(Q)`, and the two displayed adjoint maps
have integer coordinate matrices. Therefore the rational kernel extends by
scalars to the complex kernel, so its dimension over `Q` equals
`dim_C Comm(A,B)`.

**[COMPUTATION — direct exact certificate].** For the row-major `2x3` edge set

\[

\]

the stacked map `(ad_A/2,ad_B/2)` has 8192 rows, 4096 columns, 8000 nonzero
rows, and 26624 nonzero integer entries. Sparse Gaussian elimination with
`Fraction` pivots gives

\[
 \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Q}=4084,\qquad
 \operatorname{nullity}_{\mathbb Q}=12.
\]

All twelve back-substituted vectors have coefficients `+1` or `-1`; their

Every vector is substituted into every integer commutator equation exactly.
Their exact row rank is twelve. As independent checks, ranks at

## 3. Exact structure of the twelve-dimensional algebra

Let `R`, `C`, and `P` denote row reflection, column reflection, and global spin
flip. They generate the commuting group

\[
 H=\langle R,C,P\rangle\cong C_2^3.
\]

For `chi=(chi_R,chi_C,chi_P)`, let

\[
 V_\chi=\{v:Rv=(-1)^{\chi_R}v,\ Cv=(-1)^{\chi_C}v,
 Pv=(-1)^{\chi_P}v\}.
\]

**[COMPUTATION — exact sector data].** In character order
`000,001,010,011,100,101,110,111`, the sector dimensions are

\[
 14,10,6,6,6,10,6,6.
\]

Exact rational intertwiner equations give the following matrix. Its
`(chi,psi)` entry is
`dim Hom_(A,B)(V_psi,V_chi)`.

\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrrr}
 &000&001&010&011&100&101&110&111\\\hline
000&1&0&0&0&0&0&0&0\\
001&0&2&0&0&0&0&0&0\\
010&0&0&1&0&0&0&0&0\\
011&0&0&0&1&0&0&0&0\\
100&0&0&0&0&1&0&1&0\\
101&0&0&0&0&0&2&0&0\\
110&0&0&0&0&1&0&1&0\\
111&0&0&0&0&0&0&0&1
\end{array}
\]

The entries sum to twelve.

**[COMPUTATION — the two reducible sectors].** Write `s_(chi,j)` for the
stored unnormalised orbit basis in sector `chi`, with zero-based index `j`.
The exact common eigenlines are

\[
 v_{001}=s_{001,7}-s_{001,4},\quad Av_{001}=0,\quad Bv_{001}=-v_{001},
\]

and

\[
 v_{101}=s_{101,6}-s_{101,1},\quad Av_{101}=0,\quad Bv_{101}=v_{101}.
\]

Each vector has squared norm eight. Their orthogonal complements have
dimension nine and are invariant because `A` and `B` are Hermitian.

**[COMPUTATION — the repeated irreducible].** The unique intertwiner from
`V_110` to `V_100`, in the stored orbit bases, has nonzero entries

\[
 X_{0,0}=-1,\ X_{1,3}=-1,\ X_{2,1}=-1,\
 X_{3,4}=1,\ X_{4,2}=-1,\ X_{5,5}=1.
\]

It has rank six and exactly intertwines both restricted generators. Its
adjoint is the reverse intertwiner. No other off-diagonal Hom block is
nonzero.

**[LEMMA — deriving the algebra type].** A finite-dimensional unital algebra
generated by Hermitian matrices is a `*`-algebra, so its modules are completely
reducible. A diagonal commutant dimension one means the corresponding module
is irreducible. A diagonal dimension two, together with an explicit invariant
line, gives two inequivalent multiplicity-one irreducibles. A nonzero
intertwiner between two irreducibles identifies two copies of one irreducible,
whose multiplicity algebra is `M2(C)`.

Applying the lemma to the exact Hom table yields eight multiplicity-one scalar
components and one multiplicity-two component. Hence
`Comm(A,B) = C^8 direct-sum M2(C)`. An explicit structured basis is

\[
 \{P_\chi:\chi\in\mathbb F_2^3\}\cup
 \{R_{001},R_{101},X,X^\dagger\},
\]

where `P_chi` are the eight character projectors,
`R_chi=v_chi v_chi^dagger/8`, and `X` is lifted from the displayed
intertwiner. This is the same twelve-dimensional space as the explicit Pauli
basis in Section 2.

## 4. Couplings, associative closure, and the Burnside corollary

**[LEMMA — pair versus associative algebra].** If
`Alg(A,B)=<A,B>_assoc` is the unital associative algebra generated by the pair,
then

\[
 \operatorname{Comm}(A,B)=\operatorname{Comm}(\operatorname{Alg}(A,B)).
\]

*Proof.* Commuting with `A` and `B` implies commuting with every word and every
linear combination of words. The converse follows because `A,B` themselves
belong to the generated algebra. ∎

**[LEMMA — all linear couplings].** One also has

\[
 \operatorname{Comm}(A,B)
 =\bigcap_{\lambda\in\mathbb C}\operatorname{Comm}(A+\lambda B).
\]

*Proof.* The forward inclusion is immediate. For the reverse inclusion,
commute with two distinct values `lambda_0,lambda_1`; subtracting the two
commutator equations gives `[T,B]=0`, and substitution gives `[T,A]=0`. ∎

Thus the exact twelve-dimensional space is precisely the space of operators
conserved for **every** linear coupling, not the commutant at one accidentally
degenerate coupling.

**[LEMMA — conditional parity/Burnside statement].** Suppose a finite
Hermitian pair commutes with an involution `P`, and suppose

\[
 \operatorname{Comm}(A,B)=\operatorname{span}\{I,P\}.
\]

Then, with `V=V_+ direct-sum V_-`,

\[
 \operatorname{Alg}(A,B)=
 \operatorname{End}(V_+)\oplus\operatorname{End}(V_-).
\]

*Proof.* The unital algebra generated by two Hermitian matrices is a finite
`*`-algebra and therefore equals its double commutant. Under the hypothesis,
its double commutant is the commutant of `span{I,P}`, exactly the full
block-diagonal algebra preserving the two eigenspaces. Equivalently, each
parity restriction is irreducible, no intertwiner connects them, and Burnside's
theorem gives the full matrix algebra on each block. ∎

**[UNRESOLVED — antecedent fails here].** The conditional lemma is valid, but
it cannot be applied to `2x3`: the exact antecedent is false because the
commutant is twelve-dimensional. Consequently this calculation does not kill
all finite-layer conserved charges; it classifies them.

## 5. Controls and larger finite layers

**[LEMMA — why the open-chain control count is `n+1`].** Under the
Jordan--Wigner map,

\[
 X_j=i\gamma_{2j-1}\gamma_{2j},\qquad
 Z_jZ_{j+1}=i\gamma_{2j}\gamma_{2j+1}.
\]

On the one-particle Majorana space define

\[
 Je_r=(-1)^{r-1}e_{2n+1-r}.
\]

Direct substitution gives `J^2=-I` and shows that `J` commutes with both
alternating-edge quadratic forms. Its spin second quantisation therefore
commutes with `A` and `B` and has the `n+1` occupation eigenvalues
`0,1,...,n`. Its first `n+1` spectral polynomials are linearly independent
commuting operators.

**[COMPUTATION — free-fermion controls].** Direct exact Pauli elimination gives
nullities `5,6,7` for open chains `n=4,5,6`. The lemma supplies the same number
of exact independent charges, so in these three cases the full commutant is
exactly the polynomial algebra of the conserved number operator. The statement
is finite for these three sizes; an all-`n` generation theorem is not claimed
here.

**[COMPUTATION — exact large-layer block certificates].** The larger
calculations use the rational character projectors of
`H=<R,C,P>=C2^3`, followed by the exact `B`-energy restriction. In each ordered
sector pair the remaining equation is

\[
 XA_{\rm source}=A_{\rm target}X.
\]

The complete exact data are

| graph | original unknowns | variables after exact `B` restriction | restricted exact-Q rank | exact nullity |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `2x2` | 256 | 152 | 125 | 27 |
| `2x3` | 4096 | 944 | 932 | 12 |
| `2x4` | 65536 | 12560 | 12519 | 41 |
| `3x3` | 262144 | 45584 | 45486 | 98 |

For `2x4`, all 41 canonical rational block vectors are stored and every vector
is substituted exactly into its block equations. For `3x3`, all 98 are stored
and verified likewise. Thus both are exact rational results, stronger than the
requested modular-only fallback.

**[COMPUTATION — modular role, carefully scoped].** At each of the two primes
`2147483647` and `2147483629`, every full block collection has the same
nullity shown in the table. A modular rank is only a lower bound on rational
rank, hence only an upper bound on rational nullity. Equality over `Q` does not
follow from two-prime agreement alone. Here equality follows independently
from the explicit exact kernel vectors together with exact rational row
reduction (and, already from either modular upper bound plus the exact vectors,
by a two-sided dimension argument).

## 6. Independent verification

**[COMPUTATION — standalone test].** Run

```bash

```

The test does not import the experiment. It diagonalises `B` in computational

`Fraction`, verifies both commutators term by term, checks exact-basis
independence, and independently rebuilds the chain dimensions `5,6,7`.
