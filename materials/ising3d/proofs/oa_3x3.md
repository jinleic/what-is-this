# The open-`3x3` layer algebra is not an Onsager-algebra quotient

**Artifacts:** `experiments/e100_oa_3x3.py`, `tests/test_oa_3x3.py`,
`results/algebra_structure/oa_3x3.json`.
**Consumed:** `results/algebra_structure/char0_3x3.json` (wave 9, clean-room
verified by `tests/test_algebra_3x3.py`), `results/algebra_structure/oa_quotient.json`
(audited Date--Roan classification), `sources/fulltext/{date_roan2000_onsager,roan1991_onsager}.pdf`
(SHA-256 pinned in `sources/manifest.yaml`, recomputed at every run).

## 1. Statement

**[THEOREM]** Let \(\mathfrak g_{\mathbb Q}\) be the Lie algebra generated by
\(A=\sum_v X_v\) and \(B=\sum_{(u,v)\in E}Z_uZ_v\) on the open `3x3` grid, and
\(\mathfrak g_{\mathbb C}\) its complexification.  Then \(\mathfrak g_{\mathbb C}\)
is not isomorphic to any quotient of the Onsager algebra, and no pair of
elements of \(\mathfrak g_{\mathbb C}\) which generates \(\mathfrak g_{\mathbb C}\)
satisfies the normalized Dolan--Grady relations.

This note concerns this one finite graph only; no all-width or thermodynamic
assertion is made.  It extends the audited open-`2x4` corollary
(`proofs/levi_images_2x4.md` sec. 6) to the `3x3` layer, with a strictly more
robust contradiction: every one of the six simple factors independently
suffices, whereas the `2x4` argument used one certified orthogonal sector.

## 2. Certified inputs

**[THEOREM — in-repo]** (wave 9, `proofs/char0_3x3.md`)
\[
\mathfrak g_{\mathbb Q} \;=\; \mathbb Qz \,\oplus\,
\mathfrak{sl}_{33}\oplus\mathfrak{sl}_{48}\oplus\mathfrak{sl}_{59}\oplus
\mathfrak{sl}_{3}\oplus\mathfrak{sl}_{16}\oplus\mathfrak{sl}_{30}
\quad(\dim 8034),
\]
with \(\operatorname{rad}(\mathfrak g)=Z(\mathfrak g)=\mathbb Qz\) and the
derived algebra semisimple of type \(A_{32}+A_{47}+A_{58}+A_{2}+A_{15}+A_{29}\).
The factor arithmetic \(\dim=(\mathrm{rank}+1)^2-1\) and the accounting
\(1+\sum_i\dim_i=8034\) are re-verified from the raw stored integers by both
the producer and the standalone test.

**[EXTERNAL — audited]** (Date--Roan; `proofs/onsager_quotient_nogo.md`)
Every finite-dimensional semisimple quotient of the complex Onsager algebra is
a direct sum of copies of \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\); hence every Levi factor of an
arbitrary finite-dimensional Onsager quotient is a direct sum of
\(\mathfrak{sl}_2\) copies.  The classification passes to the closed kernel of
the semisimple quotient; it does not assume arbitrary kernels are closed.

## 3. Proof

Since \(\operatorname{rad}(\mathfrak g_{\mathbb Q})=\mathbb Qz\), the quotient
\(\mathfrak g_{\mathbb Q}\twoheadrightarrow\mathfrak g_{\mathbb Q}/\mathbb Qz\)
is the displayed split semisimple direct sum \(\mathfrak s\).  A semisimple
algebra surjects onto each of its simple ideals by projecting along the
complementary ideal, so for each \(i\) there is an exact surjection
\(\mathfrak g_{\mathbb Q}\twoheadrightarrow\mathfrak{sl}_{d_i}(\mathbb Q)\),
\(d_i\in\{33,48,59,3,16,30\}\).  Scalar extension is right exact, giving
\[
 \rho_i:\mathfrak g_{\mathbb C}\twoheadrightarrow\mathfrak{sl}_{d_i}(\mathbb C),
\]
a surjection onto a simple algebra of type \(A_{d_i-1}\) with \(d_i\ge 3\) —
never \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\).

If \(\mathfrak g_{\mathbb C}\) were an Onsager quotient, then
\(\mathfrak{sl}_{d_i}(\mathbb C)\) would be a further quotient, i.e. a
finite-dimensional semisimple quotient of the Onsager algebra, hence a direct
sum of \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\) copies by the audited corollary.  A *simple* direct
sum of \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\) copies is \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\) itself, of dimension
3; but \(\dim\mathfrak{sl}_{d_i}(\mathbb C)=d_i^2-1\ge 8\).  Contradiction.

Finally, any generating pair of \(\mathfrak g_{\mathbb C}\) satisfying the
normalized Dolan--Grady relations would induce a surjection from the universal
Onsager presentation onto \(\mathfrak g_{\mathbb C}\), exhibiting it as an
Onsager quotient — the same contradiction.  \(\square\)

## 4. Scope and honesty

- The theorem is unconditional **given** the two certified inputs; both are
  re-verified from disk (embedded checks re-evaluated, SHA-256 recomputed) on
  every producer run and independently by `tests/test_oa_3x3.py`.
- Consistency, not surprise: the repository's Dolan--Grady defect theorem
  already obstructs the *specific* generating pair \((A,B)\) on any graph with
  a degree-\(\ge 3\) vertex.  The present corollary is strictly stronger for
  the `3x3` layer: it excludes **every** generating pair and **every**
  quotient realization, after arbitrary changes of generators.
- Nothing here bears on infinite-dimensional or non-Onsager integrability
  mechanisms.
