Exact transcription (line breaks normalized; `P` is the survey's printed

"Theorem 6.1.1 (Strassen [52]). Let 3 ≤ a ≤ b = c ≤ r. Let
T ∈ σ_r(Seg(PA × PB × PC)) and α ∈ A* be such that
T_α := T(α) ∈ B⊗C, considered as a map T_α:C*→B, is of full rank.
For each α¹,α² ∈ A*, define the linear map T_{α,α_j}:B→B by
T_{α,α_j}=T_{α_j}T_α^{-1}. Then
Rank[T_{α,α_1},T_{α,α_2}] ≤ 2(r−b),
where [S,T]=ST−TS is the commutator of endomorphisms."

Application to this campaign:
The survey works over the complex numbers.  Any real rank-r decomposition
complexifies to complex border rank at most r, so its inequality is still a
valid lower-bound obstruction for real rank.
For p=3 and b=c=8, a full-rank slice exists whenever u != 0.  The matrices
T_{alpha,alpha_j}=L_v L_u^{-1} are simultaneously conjugate by L_u to the
campaign's L_u^{-1}L_v=N(u)^{-1}L_bar(u)L_v, so commutator rank is unchanged.

independent (u,v,w).  The quoted inequality therefore yields

yields 4 <= 2(r-4), i.e. r >= 6, consistent with true rank 7.  The naked
r >= b+rank(commutator) reading would yield 8 at tau and is refuted.
