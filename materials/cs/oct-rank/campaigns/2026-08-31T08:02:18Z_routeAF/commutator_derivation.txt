ROUTE A UNIVERSAL COMMUTATOR DERIVATION

Evidence strength: HUMAN ALGEBRAIC PROOF, anchored by exact coefficient checks
in commutator_proof.out.  The script checks the two universal quadratic matrix
identities coefficient by coefficient, but the deductions below are written
out here and are not overlabelled as wholly machine-proved.

Convention and domain.
Use real Cayley--Dickson octonions with positive norm N(x)=<x,x> in the ordered
basis (1,i,j,k,l,il,jl,kl).  L_x[c,b]=(x*e_b)_c.  Let u,v,w be linearly
independent real octonions.  Put U=N(u)>0, uv=<u,v>, uw=<u,w>, and vw=<v,w>.
The exact basis-coefficient anchors establish, for all real x,y by bilinearity,

  L_x^T = L_conj(x),
  L_conj(x)L_y + L_conj(y)L_x = 2<x,y>I.                 (1)

In particular L_conj(x)L_x=L_xL_conj(x)=N(x)I.  No
replacement of a product of left-multiplication matrices by one octonion
product is made; that would be invalid outside an associative subalgebra.

Orthogonal decomposition.
Set

  a=uv/U,       v_perp=v-a u,
  b=uw/U,       w_perp=w-b u.

Then <u,v_perp>=<u,w_perp>=0.  Define

  P=U^{-1} L_conj(u)L_vperp,
  Q=U^{-1} L_conj(u)L_wperp,
  alpha=N(v_perp)/U=(U N(v)-uv^2)/U^2,
  beta =N(w_perp)/U=(U N(w)-uw^2)/U^2,
  gamma=<v_perp,w_perp>/U=(U vw-uv uw)/U^2.

Let X=U^{-1}L_conj(u)L_v=aI+P and
Y=U^{-1}L_conj(u)L_w=bI+Q.  Scalar parts commute, so [X,Y]=[P,Q].

Consequences of (1).
Apply (1) to conjugated arguments to obtain
L_z L_conj(u)=-L_u L_conj(z) whenever <u,z>=0.  Therefore

  P^2=-U^{-2}L_conj(u)L_u L_conj(v_perp)L_vperp
     =-alpha I,
  Q^2=-beta I.

Using the same relation once in each product and then (1),

  PQ+QP
   =-U^{-1}(L_conj(v_perp)L_wperp
             +L_conj(w_perp)L_vperp)
   =-2 gamma I.                                          (2)

Commutator square.
Let C=[P,Q].  Equation (2) gives QP=-2 gamma I-PQ and hence

  C=PQ-QP=2(PQ+gamma I).

Moreover

  (PQ+gamma I)^2
   =P(QP)Q+2 gamma PQ+gamma^2 I
   =-P^2Q^2+gamma^2 I
   =(gamma^2-alpha beta)I.

Thus

  C^2=-4(alpha beta-gamma^2)I.                           (3)

Adding multiples of u to v and w does not change the Gram determinant.  Its
orthogonal block form gives

  det Gram(u,v,w)
    =U (N(v_perp)N(w_perp)-<v_perp,w_perp>^2),

so alpha beta-gamma^2=det Gram(u,v,w)/U^3.  If
D=[L_conj(u)L_v,L_conj(u)L_w], then D=U^2 C, and (3) rescales to

  D^2=-4 U det Gram(u,v,w) I.                            (4)

Why this is invertible on exactly the declared domain.
The octonion norm inner product is positive definite over R.  Its Gram
determinant is strictly positive exactly when u,v,w are linearly independent,
and is zero exactly when they are dependent.  Therefore on every independent
real triple, the scalar in (4) is strictly negative and nonzero, D is
invertible, and rank(D)=8.  For a dependent triple (with an invertible slice
chosen where possible), (4) gives D^2=0, hence image(D) is contained in
kernel(D) and rank(D)<=4; such triples cannot improve the universal floor.

Order and conjugacy to the imported theorem.
With the slice convention above, identify the theorem's input columns and
output rows so T_alpha=L_u.  Landsberg Theorem 6.1.1 uses
T_{alpha,alpha_j}=T_{alpha_j}T_alpha^{-1}=L_v L_u^{-1}.  The matrices used
above are X=L_u^{-1}L_v and Y=L_u^{-1}L_w, because
L_u^{-1}=U^{-1}L_conj(u).  They have the same commutator rank: explicitly

  L_vL_u^{-1}=L_u X L_u^{-1},
  L_wL_u^{-1}=L_u Y L_u^{-1},

so the theorem-order commutator is L_u[X,Y]L_u^{-1}.  This establishes the
order convention rather than relying on letter transcription.

Imported lower-bound conclusion.
The theorem import is CITED-DEPENDENCY; Strassen-primary provenance is
UNESTABLISHED.  Landsberg's exact inequality is

  rank([T_{alpha,alpha_1},T_{alpha,alpha_2}]) <= 2(r-b).

Here b=8 and the human proof above gives commutator rank 8, so
8<=2(r-8), equivalently r>=8+8/2=12.  It does not yield 16.  The quaternion
tau calibration has b=4 and commutator rank 4, hence only r>=4+4/2=6,
consistent with (but not proving) true rank 7.  The calibration's role is to
refute the missing-factor reading r>=b+rank(commutator), which would falsely
produce 8.
