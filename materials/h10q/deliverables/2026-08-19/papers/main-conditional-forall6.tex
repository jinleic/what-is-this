\begin{abstract}
Assuming classical Schinzel's Hypothesis~H, we prove that $\Z$ has a
definition in $\Q$ with six universal quantifiers and that
$\operatorname{efd}_{\Q}(\Q\setminus\Z)\le5$.  This is a conditional
result, not an unconditional solution of Hilbert's Tenth Problem over
$\Q$.  For every cell $(w,z)$, the class construction fixes
\[
 \tau^\dagger=\frac{1+2a^2}{1+4a^2},
 \qquad \delta_{\tau^\dagger}=-\frac{4a^4}{1+4a^2},
\]
uses $f=w$, and proves normalization, coprimality, absence of a fixed
divisor, frozen local data, and positivity uniformly.
% src: data/l19_classexist.jsonl; data/l20_admissible.jsonl

The last non-Schinzel obligation is removed by a fixed-fiber theorem.  For
every fixed $Z\in\Q^\times$, equal endpoints and Newton polygons at $A$,
$a=0$, and $a=\infty$ eliminate every possible factor of
\[
 H=\frac A4P
 =a^8Z^4N_g^2+4A^3D^2b^4-2A^4(a-1)^2D^2b^5.
\]
The degree-four case would force
$Z^4=1024(1-Z)^2$, whose two quadratic branches have nonsquare
discriminants $1152$ and $896$; the degenerate value $Z=1$ has an exact
degree-eight certificate modulo $11$.  Hence $P(a,Z)$ is irreducible in
$\Q(a)[b]$ for every nonzero rational $Z$.
% src: data/l22_elimination.jsonl (fixed-Z-theorem and exceptional-fiber rows)

Quantitative Hilbert irreducibility selects a good $a$ inside the existing
character-admissible progression.  The L20 class and the L19 fixed-pair
implication then reduce member existence exactly to classical Schinzel~H;
the resulting simultaneous prime values give an emergent-free member and
activate L6.  An independent reciprocal-trace and complete
$2$-isogeny-descent proof at $a=1$ corroborates the vertical theorem;
that $a=1$ is a specialization witness, not the selected class parameter.
Thus Schinzel~H is the sole conjectural input and no per-cell
irreducibility hypothesis remains.
% src: data/l22_reciprocal_cube.jsonl (summary row)
% src: data/l20_admissible.jsonl; data/l18_schinzel_implies_h.jsonl

The L23 half-sieve gives, unconditionally, \(\gg
X/(\log X)^{3/2}\) prime-sequence candidates clean at every bad prime
below \(X^{49/100}\).  These are small-prime-clean members of the
progression, not full emergent-free members: degree-eight values may retain
up to \(16\) large factors, and reciprocity leaves \(0,2,\ldots,16\) bad
ones.  L24 proves that both proposed \emph{unscaled} self-coupled diagonal
orientations are \(\Phi\)-empty over \(\Q_2\).  L25 shows that fixed
scaling removes this universal dyadic wall but leaves parity and target
compatibility in those formulas.  L26 gives an even-pullback reciprocal
tie with a quartic trace character.  L27 gives one triangular shear with
complete dyadic and aligned odd-target local points.  No reciprocal
member, rational coupled point, or five-count improvement is claimed,
and none of these results changes the conditional theorem.
% src: data/l23_half_sieve.jsonl; data/l24_diagonal_arithmetic.jsonl
% src: data/l25_scaled_coupling.jsonl; data/l26_reciprocal_tie.jsonl
% src: data/l27_triangular_shear.jsonl
\end{abstract}

\section{Conclusion and outlook}

For every nonzero rational $Z$, the fixed canonical branch
$\tau^\dagger=(1+2a^2)/(1+4a^2)$,
$\delta_{\tau^\dagger}=-4a^4/(1+4a^2)$ is vertically irreducible.  The
endpoint--Newton proof is uniform in the fixed fiber, including the exact
$Z=1$ certificate; quantitative Hilbert irreducibility then chooses a good
$a$ inside the character progression already required by the class
construction.
% src: data/l22_elimination.jsonl (fixed-Z-theorem and exceptional-fiber rows)

The logical chain is therefore complete up to one named conjecture:
fixed-$Z$ irreducibility gives Hilbert selection in the admissible
$a$ progression; the L20 class certificate has $f=w$ and all
Schinzel-pair conditions; the L19 fixed-pair implication turns classical
Schinzel~H into infinitely many emergent-free members; and L6 yields
\[
 \Z\ \text{universally definable in }\Q\text{ with six quantifiers},
 \qquad
 \operatorname{efd}_{\Q}(\Q\setminus\Z)\le5.
\]
% src: data/l20_admissible.jsonl; data/l18_schinzel_implies_h.jsonl
% src: CONDITIONAL.md:14-48

The independent reciprocal proof specializes to
\[
 (a,A,s,\tau^\dagger,\delta)=(1,5,0,3/5,-4/5),
\]
factors through a trace quartic, and closes the reciprocal lift by a
complete $2$-isogeny descent.
Its $a=1$ is only a specialization witness for the rational-function
fiber; it need not satisfy the target character condition and is not the
class parameter chosen by Hilbert irreducibility.
% src: data/l22_reciprocal_cube.jsonl (meta and summary rows)

The alternate routes retain their honest scope without weakening the
primary theorem.  Infinity alone permits factor degrees $2$, $4$, and
$6$; factor tuples control only the product of outside signs without an
all-plus compatibility theorem; and a free $\lambda$ moves $\tau$ through
an infinite L11c family and would add a seventh witness.  None is used as
the six-count closure.
% src: data/l22_infinity.jsonl; data/l22_factor_tuple.jsonl
% src: data/l22_square_branch.jsonl

L23 sharpens the unconditional frontier but does not cross it.  Its
half-dimensional beta sieve proves a positive lower bound
\(\gg X/(\log X)^{3/2}\) for candidates clean at every bad prime below
\(X^{49/100}\).  It proves no positive lower bound for fully clean members:
the degree-eight cofactor can retain up to \(16\) large factors and parity
only forces the bad count to be even.  The first missing estimate is the
two-large-bad-divisor sector beyond Bombieri--Vinogradov.  Known
prime-argument almost-prime theorems, even Kao's \(P_{12}\), do not reach
the required \(R_{\rm bad}\le1\).
% src: data/l23_half_sieve.jsonl

The same audit closes several algebraic escape routes.  On the refined
class, the conic bundle has non-split rank \(10\) and
\(\operatorname{Br}(X)/\operatorname{Br}(\Q)\cong\Z/2\Z\), generated by
\((A,b)\); the rank-\(\le2\) fibration theorem does not apply.  Exact norm
matching and the loci \(P=\pm2b\) times a square miss \(\Phi\), no
polynomial-in-\(b\) norm section exists, and fixed twists only move the
character obligation.  The factor branch \(L=-A\rho^2\) leads to scoped
Hilbert symbols and genus-four curves, not a global section.
% src: data/l23_fibration.jsonl; data/l23_norm_section.jsonl
% src: data/l23_squareclass.jsonl; data/l23_rational_section.jsonl

L24 decisively refutes the unscaled diagonal five-count.  Both exact
finite-flat degree-eight unit orientations have empty image on
\(\Phi(\Q_2)\): orientation I has root valuations \(0\) or \(4\),
incompatible with the two required squares, while orientation II reduces
to a complete contradiction modulo \(16\).  Odd target places are locally
soluble in the standard \(k=1\) stratum, so this is a genuinely dyadic
obstruction.  The \(4{,}026{,}282\) zero-hit search is corroborating
EVIDENCE only, not the proof.
% src: data/l24_diagonal_arithmetic.jsonl; data/l24_diagonal_local.jsonl
% src: data/l24_diagonal_search.jsonl

L25 separates that fact from a universal coupling wall.  Its two scaled
type-I formulas cover complementary dyadic parities, while their smooth
target formula is on a freely chosen unit-\(b\) stratum rather than the
aligned L20 stratum.  L26 changes the tie: \(Z=z^2\) and
\(\eta=Z(b+1)/(Db)\) make the tied polynomial reciprocal with a quartic
trace character, and remove its dyadic symbol.  L27 instead uses the
single shear
\[
 (y,r)=(2X+28\rho,\ sX+\rho).
\]
One strong-Hensel argument covers all dyadic parities, and a Weil
character-sum theorem, with \(43\) exact small-prime rows, supplies a
smooth point on every standard aligned odd target.  The remaining
degree-eight global cover has no proved rational point.  No member
theorem or conditional five-count follows.
% src: data/l25_scaled_coupling.jsonl; data/l26_reciprocal_tie.jsonl
% src: data/l27_triangular_shear.jsonl

Accordingly, classical Schinzel's Hypothesis~H remains the sole
conjectural input to the established six-universal-quantifier definition.
The exact next frontiers are the L26 quartic trace squareclass/member
problem, a target-specific rational point on L27's global octic, or a
parity-sensitive two-large-divisor estimate for the linear--octic
sequence.  The theorem remains conditional: we make no new unconditional
quantifier-record claim and do not claim that H10(\(\Q\)) is solved.
% src: data/litscout_h10q.md; data/l23_half_sieve.jsonl
% src: data/l26_reciprocal_tie.jsonl; data/l27_triangular_shear.jsonl

\section*{Reproducibility}

All certificates in this paper are produced by a stdlib-only kernel with
a proven-primality discipline: deterministic Miller--Rabin below
$3.3\times 10^{24}$, Pocklington with the Brillhart--Lehmer--Selfridge
relaxation above, and explicit refusal labels that never count as
evidence. The companion paper documents the engine, the artifact
schemas, the exact reproduction commands, and the precise scope of what
is regenerable from checked-in scripts.
