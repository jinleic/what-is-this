## What was decided

For each code, `V_w` is the GF(2) span of all stabilizers of symplectic weight at most `w`, and `w*` is the least `w` with `rank(V_w) = n - k = 132`. Two independent proof routes are recorded per PBB member:

- `exact_closure`: the closure loop terminated, so `rank(V_7)` is an exact value. Closure is either a full-rowspace span or a CP-SAT `INFEASIBLE` against a complete basis of physical annihilator functionals.
- `gate_plus_structural`: the decisive query `exists v in rowspace(H): symplectic_weight(v) <= 7 and X-part nonzero` returned `INFEASIBLE`, so `V_7` sits inside the pure-Z stabilizer subgroup, whose exact rank `r_Zsub` was computed by plain GF(2) linear algebra (no solver) and is strictly below 132.
