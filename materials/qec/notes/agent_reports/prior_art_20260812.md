### arXiv:2603.21499 - "Optimal Compilation of Syndrome Extraction Circuits for
### General Quantum LDPC Codes" (ASC), v1 2026-03-23. FULL TEXT CHECKED.
- SMT-based depth-optimal compiler; one ancilla per stabilizer (Hadamard-test
  form, CX/CZ); iteratively certifies depth optimality.
- **Certifies no depth-6 syndrome extraction for all BB instances in IBM's
  work; produces depth-7 within 2h.** This scoops the bare FACT of our Theorem
  C3 for those instances (they are first, 2026-03). Our C3 must cite ASC and be
  repositioned as: independent CP-SAT confirmation, extended to all 47 catalogue
  BB codes, PLUS the analytic anticommuting-overlap parity criterion that
  explains WHY 6 fails - ASC has no analytic criterion (their only lower bound,
  Sec III-B, is "obvious" Tanner max degree Delta(G), which does NOT explain the
  6->7 gap; their evidence is solver-certificate-only).
- Their Delta(G) lower-bound claim is exactly the premise our E22a counterexample
  shows is model-scoped: valid only without ancilla-ancilla gates (parity
  forwarding beats it). Their model excludes such gates, so no contradiction -
  but our scoping analysis is sharper and worth stating.
- Framework claims non-CSS (BSF) generality (Sec III-D) but every compiled
  example (BB, GB, HGP, color) is CSS. No mixed-stabilizer instance compiled;
  no PBB; no generating-set optimization. Claims 1, 5 untouched; claim 3
  overlaps only qualitatively (their LER gains are cross-depth ASAP/coloration
  comparisons, not within-minimum-depth-class schedule spread).
- Open source: github.com/iqubit-org/Auto-Stabilizer-Check (not yet cloned; do
  so before any circuit-level comparison against ASC schedules).
