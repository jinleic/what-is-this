**Liu H2 holds for the paired class**: every `q in [0,1]`, every mass vector, every admissible
`M >= m`, arbitrarily many atoms, no support restriction. Liu's Hypothesis 2 as stated
(Section V-B, nine parameters: three shared masses, six supports, `q`; `uc/liu9_objective.py`)
is the three-atom case. Because `_formula` depends on the configuration only through the marginals

product coupling (`n m` atoms with masses `a_i b_j`), so the finitely supported form is covered
outright. Extension to non-atomic laws is a weak-density remark that is **not written or checked**.
